Wednesday, August 5

Celebration of 36 Years of Ministry

Thanksgiving & Intercession for HIMU

His Faithful Love Continues Forever

"At that time long ago, the Lord showed himself to his people. He said, ‘I have always loved you and I always will love you, my faithful love for you continues forever.’"

Jeremiah 31:3 (EASY)

  • Thank God for 36 years of His unfailing love and faithfulness to HIMU
  • Thank God that this faithful love abides with HIMU forever

Write the Vision Plainly

"Then the Lord said to me, ‘Write my answer plainly on tablets, so that a runner can carry the correct message to others.’"

Habakkuk 2:2 (NLT)

  • Thank God for Bishop and Lady Chrys for choosing not to be refugees but missionaries
  • Thank God for long healthy life full of His goodness, wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, and the fear of the Lord as they continue to lead HIMU
  • Declare the vision: "A world where no one lives or dies without Christ"

Holding Up the Hands of Leadership

"When Moses' arms grew tired, Aaron and Hur brought a stone for him to sit on, while they stood beside him and held up his arms, holding them steady until the sun went down. In this way Joshua totally defeated the Amalekites."

Exodus 17:12-14 (Good News)

  • Thank God for the six HIMU Diocesan Bishops and First Ladies who continue to lift the hands of Bishop and Lady Chrys to accomplish the Vision and Mission
  • Declare HIMU shall continue to enlarge their tent pegs and conquer new territories as Leadership works together in unity
  • Declare that they lack nothing good as they labor in the Lord's vineyard

A House of Prayer for All Nations

"…because my Temple will be called a house of prayer for all nations."

Isaiah 56:7b (NLT)

HIMU Vision is to make Christ known and believed in — in all nations

  • Declare that HIMU is a House of Prayer for all nations
  • Declare that we shall commit to corporately and individually pray for nations consistently
The 10/40 Window

The Resistant Belt

North Africa, Middle East & Asia

Let the Light Shine on Them

"But even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled to those who are perishing, whose minds the god of this age has blinded, who do not believe, lest the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine on them."

2 Corinthians 4:3-4 (NKJV)

  • Pray for Muslims, Hindus, and Buddhists to come to the saving knowledge of Jesus

The Harvest Is Plentiful

"Then He said to His disciples, ‘The harvest truly is plentiful, but the laborers are few. Therefore, pray the Lord of the harvest to send out laborers into His harvest.’"

Matthew 9:37-38 (NKJV)

  • Pray that more laborers be released to bring the Gospel to them
  • Pray for church-planting movements to be started and multiplied among the unreached

Boldness, Signs & Wonders

"Now, Lord, look on their threats, and grant to Your servants that with all boldness they may speak Your word, by stretching out Your hand to heal, and that signs and wonders may be done through the name of Your holy Servant Jesus."

Acts 4:29-30 (NKJV)

  • Pray for boldness to speak the Word and for signs and wonders to be done in the Name of Jesus
Post-Christian Societies

Turned Back to the Lord

Europe, USA & Canada

From Darkness to Light

"…to open their eyes, in order to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan to God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among those who are sanctified by faith in Me."

Acts 26:18 (NKJV)

  • Pray that the hearts of many in Europe and the Western world in general be turned to the Lord

Christ, the Power & Wisdom of God

"But we preach Christ crucified, to Jews a stumbling block, and to the Greeks foolishness, but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God."

1 Corinthians 1:23-24 (NKJV)

  • Pray for many laborers to be released to preach and teach the truth boldly
  • Pray for strong Word-based churches to multiply
Thursday, August 6

HIMU Celebration of 36 Years of Ministry

Prayer & Fasting

A Hundredfold Harvest

"Then Isaac sowed in that land and reaped in the same year a hundredfold; and the Lord blessed him."

Genesis 26:12 (NKJV)

  • Thank God for continuously enlarging HIMU from 1991 (Howard University) to Olney
  • Declare that He will continue to show us more and take us further than we have ever been before

Declarations

Season of Breakthrough

Declare it is our season of "Breakthrough"

Exceedingly Abundantly

Declare that God will do exceedingly abundantly above all we ask or think, according to the power that works in us in all aspects of the Ministry

The Army Is Being Formed

Declare that the Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun army is being formed at Harvest 60

The 4/14 Window

Children From 4 to 14 Years Old

Reaching the Next Generation

Let the Little Children Come

"Then little children were brought to Him that He might put His hands on them and pray, but the disciples rebuked them. But Jesus said, ‘Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of heaven.’"

Matthew 19:13-14 (NKJV)

  • Pray that the Church prioritizes reaching children and youth — that many children and youth in the nations come to know Jesus as Lord
  • Pray for Godly strategies to reach a generation — youth and children ministries, academic institutions, sports, marketplace

He Makes Wars Cease

"He makes wars cease to the end of the earth; He breaks the bow and cuts the spear in two; He burns the chariot in the fire."

Psalm 46:9 (NKJV)

  • Pray for the areas of the world where active conflicts and unrest are taking place — Ukraine, Democratic Republic of Congo, Iran and the Middle East, Niger, Mali, and others
  • Pray for divine intervention in these conflicts

A Tower of Refuge

"But you are a tower of refuge to the poor, O Lord, a tower of refuge to the needy in distress. You are a refuge from the storm and a shelter from the heat."

Isaiah 25:4a (NLT)

  • Pray for those facing famine and starvation around the world, and for refugees and internally displaced peoples
  • Pray that the Church takes the opportunity to minister to both the physical and spiritual needs of the suffering
  • Pray for wisdom, favor, and resources for Christian relief organizations, and that Christians around the world give generously to meet the humanitarian needs of the destitute

The Persecuted Church

"Do not fear any of those things which you are about to suffer… Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life. He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. He who overcomes shall not be hurt by the second death."

Revelation 2:10-11 (NKJV)

  • Pray for the believers who are being persecuted for their faith — that God will strengthen, encourage, and comfort them, and that their faith will not fail because of trials
  • Pray that if possible, God will make a way of escape for them
Ask For The Nations

The Ends of the Earth

Your Inheritance & Your Possession

Ask of Me

"Ask of Me, and I will give You the nations for Your inheritance, and the ends of the earth for Your possession."

Psalm 2:8 (NKJV)

  • Pray that the Good News of the Kingdom, the Gospel of Grace, be preached in all nations
  • Ask that all nations — tribes, tongues, and peoples — have an opportunity to hear the Gospel and that many will be saved

A Witness to All Nations

"And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come."

Matthew 24:14 (NKJV)

  • Pray that Harvest Intercontinental Ministries Unlimited and the global Body of Christ make use of all means to get the Word out — Radio, Television, Internet, Satellite, Print
  • Pray that we will see and grasp the urgency of the hour and run with the vision

Go and Make Disciples

"Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

Matthew 28:19-20 (NKJV)

  • Pray that many missionaries be raised up and released into the nations
  • Pray for all believers to rise up as marketplace ministers in their workplaces, communities, and other places of influence

November 2026 Prayer Points

Praying for HIM-U, the Nations and Harvest Olney

Wednesday, November 4 • Thursday, November 5

Harvest Intercontinental Church, Olney

Wednesday, November 4

Praying for HIM-U and the Nations

Begin with the following declarations

Opening Declarations

One Spirit with Him

I am united with the Lord, one spirit with Him! 1 Corinthians 6:17

Nothing Can Separate Me

I cannot be separated from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus my Lord. Romans 8:39

Before We Call, He Answers

Before we call, He will answer; and while we are still speaking, He will hear. Isaiah 65:24

Work for the Peace of the City

"And work for the peace and prosperity of the city where I sent you into exile. Pray to the LORD for it, for its welfare will determine your welfare."

Jeremiah 29:7 (NLT)

"By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted, but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked."

Proverbs 11:11 (NKJV)

Praying for America and the Nations

  • Pray for America, that God will give wisdom to those in authority and to those who advise them
  • Pray that America will experience more peace and prosperity
  • Pray the same for each of the nations where HIM-U is established

Praying Jeremiah 29:7 and Proverbs 11:11

Wisdom for Those Who Lead

"If you need wisdom, ask our generous God, and he will give it to you. He will not rebuke you for asking."

James 1:5 (NLT)

  • Thank God for this wisdom He generously provides to all without finding fault
  • Pray for Bishop Johnson, Pastor Chrys, all HIM-U Bishops and their wives, that God give them wisdom as they lead HIM-U

Rescued from Every Evil Action

"And the Lord will rescue me from every evil action and bring me safely into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen."

2 Timothy 4:18 (BSB)

  • Pray for God's continuous protection, provision, and leading upon all HIM-U Pastors, Ministers, Elders, Deacons and their spouses
  • Declare that the Lord will rescue them from every evil action and no weapon formed against them shall prosper

In Demonstration of the Spirit and of Power

"And my speech and my preaching was not with enticing words of man's wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power: that your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God."

1 Corinthians 2:4-5 (KJV)

  • Pray that this be the case as Bishop teaches: not with his own wisdom but with a demonstration of the power of the Spirit

Go and Make Disciples

"Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Teach these new disciples to obey all the commands I have given you. And be sure of this: I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

Matthew 28:19-20 (NLT)

  • Pray that God would ignite a fresh passion for the Great Commission in every heart
  • Pray that all HIM-U churches around the world will come into alignment with the vision God has given HIM-U: a world where no one lives or dies without Christ

Harvesters Shape Nations

  • Pray that every Harvester will fully embrace the Harvest vision and run with it
  • Declare as Harvesters we shape nations and rescue generations around the world. No continent excluded. No people group forgotten. No culture ignored. No generation abandoned.

Continuing in Matthew 28:19-20

I Will Make You Fishers of Men

"As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. ‘Come, follow Me,’ Jesus said, ‘and I will make you fishers of men.’ And at once they left their nets and followed Him."

Mark 1:16-18 (BSB)

  • Pray that God will do a deep work in our hearts; that He will make us fishers of men, effective Harvesters

The Harvest Is Plentiful

"Jesus went through all the towns and villages, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness. When He saw the crowds, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd. Then He said to His disciples, ‘The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest.’"

Matthew 9:35-38 (BSB)

  • Pray that God will fill our hearts with compassion for souls
  • Ask the Lord of the harvest to send out workers into His harvest

Abounding in Every Good Work

"And God is able to bless you abundantly, so that in all things at all times, having all that you need, you will abound in every good work."

2 Corinthians 9:8 (NIV)

  • Pray for an abundance of resources for every good work the Lord has called HIM-U to do
  • Pray that HIM-U members will be faithful to give generously to support HIM-U projects
Thursday, November 5

Praying for Harvest Olney

Begin with the following declarations

Opening Declarations

  • Declare as we declare the Word over our situations, the Word will always produce fruit. It will always accomplish what God wants it to, and it will always prosper everywhere He sends it. Isaiah 55:11
  • Declare our God is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us. Ephesians 3:20
  • Declare all things work together for our good. We are more than conquerors through Him that loves us. Nothing can separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord. Romans 8:28, 37, 39

Led by the Spirit of God

"There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit."

Romans 8:1 (NKJV)

"For if you live according to the flesh you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God."

Romans 8:13-14 (NKJV)

I Am Free

  • Declare Thank God! Through Jesus Christ, I am in the Spirit, the Spirit lives in me, and I belong to Christ. I no longer have to obey the desires of the flesh. I am free.
  • Declare the Holy Spirit leads me to wage war against sin. Sin is an exception, not the rule in my life.
  • Pray that all Harvesters will receive a deeper revelation of Jesus through His Word

Praying Romans 8:1, 13-14

Deliver Us from the Evil One

"And do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one. For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever. Amen."

Matthew 6:13 (NKJV)

  • Pray that all Harvesters will choose to follow the Holy Spirit's guidance and will depend on God's strength to overcome temptation
  • Pray that all Harvesters will trust Him to deliver them when tempted

Spend and Be Spent for Souls

"And I will very gladly spend and be spent for your souls; though the more abundantly I love you, the less I am loved."

2 Corinthians 12:15 (NKJV)

  • Declare I thank God, I am not called merely to be a disciple. I am called to become a mature disciple of Jesus Christ who is capable of, and responsible for, making disciples.
  • Pray that God will give all Harvesters in Olney and around the world willing hearts to spend and be spent for souls

Gladly Investing in the Harvest

  • Pray that we may gladly invest our time, strength, gifts, and resources in the spiritual growth of our local church and in reaching our communities and the world for Christ
  • Pray that God will deepen our love for those we are called to serve, and that we will be empowered to love faithfully even when our love is unrecognized or unreturned

Continuing in 2 Corinthians 12:15

Put Away Childish Things

"When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I understood as a child, I thought as a child; but when I became a man, I put away childish things."

1 Corinthians 13:11 (NKJV)

"For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to teach you again the first principles of the oracles of God; and you have come to need milk and not solid food."

Hebrews 5:12 (NKJV)

Growing into Maturity

  • Declare by the grace of God, I am putting away spiritual immaturity and growing into a mature disciple of Christ. I take responsibility for my part in fulfilling the Great Commission.
  • Declare my feelings will not govern my obedience to God. His Word directs my choices, and His Spirit empowers me to remain faithful to His work.
  • Pray that all Harvesters will embrace their assignment of making disciples for Christ

Praying 1 Corinthians 13:11 and Hebrews 5:12

Totally Committed to Good Deeds

"He gave his life to free us from every kind of sin, to cleanse us, and to make us his very own people, totally committed to doing good deeds."

Titus 2:14 (NLT)

"Never be lazy, but work hard and serve the Lord enthusiastically."

Romans 12:11 (NLT)

  • Pray that all Harvesters will grow in zeal and serve the Lord enthusiastically

The Fire of God Burning in Me

  • Declare Jesus gave His life to free me from every kind of sin and make me His own. I am cleansed, set apart, and eager to do good!
  • Declare I belong to God's own people, totally committed to doing good deeds.
  • Declare I am in Christ and He lives in me. He is the fire of God working in me, the zeal of God burning in me now.

Praying Titus 2:14 and Romans 12:11

You in Me, and I in You

"At that day you will know that I am in My Father, and you in Me, and I in you."

John 14:20 (NKJV)

  • Declare that God has supplied all of our needs through our union with Christ Jesus
  • Declare that God is sufficient. His life in us is sufficient for every challenge we are facing now or will face tomorrow
  • Thank God that we can endure all things because of the strength He supplies through our union with Christ

Overwhelming Victory Is Ours

"No, despite all these things, overwhelming victory is ours through Christ, who loved us."

Romans 8:37 (NLT)

"But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ, and through us spreads and makes evident everywhere the sweet fragrance of the knowledge of Him."

2 Corinthians 2:14 (AMP)

  • Declare God is always at work in us, causing us to triumph in Christ
  • Declare overwhelming victory is ours through Christ, who loved us

He Has Heard and Answered

"Before we call, He will answer; and while we are still speaking, He will hear."

Isaiah 65:24

Closing

Thank God for the assurance that as we, the righteous, have prayed in agreement in the name of Jesus according to the Word of God, He has heard and answered. Amen.

Harvest Intercontinental Church, Olney • November 4 and 5, 2026

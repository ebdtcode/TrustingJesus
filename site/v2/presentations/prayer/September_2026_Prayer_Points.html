Praying for HIM-U Olney

A Brand-New Day for Harvest Olney

Behold, I Will Do a New Thing

"Behold, I will do a new thing, now it shall spring forth; shall you not know it? I will even make a road in the wilderness and rivers in the desert."

Isaiah 43:19 (NKJV)

  • Pray that we will recognize that it is a brand-new day and shift completely into purpose
  • Pray that we will understand the urgency of the time, that there is no time for delay, and flow with the Lord's agenda

Holy, Holy, Holy

"In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and the train of His robe filled the temple. Above it stood seraphim; each one had six wings: with two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew. And one cried to another and said: ‘Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory!’"

Isaiah 6:1-4 (NKJV)

  • Pray that we will get a deeper revelation of the holiness of God like Isaiah, and that a revelation of His holiness will lead to a deeper reverence for God and obedience to His will

Separated, Sent, and Released

"…As they ministered to the Lord and fasted, the Holy Spirit said, ‘Now separate to Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.’ Then, having fasted and prayed, and laid hands on them, they sent them away. So, being sent out by the Holy Spirit, they went down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed to Cyprus."

Acts 13:1-4 (NKJV)

  • Pray that Harvest Olney will become more and more an apostolic hub where harvesters will be trained and released into the field, locally, regionally, and globally

The Latter House of Greater Glory

"‘The glory of this latter temple shall be greater than the former,’ says the Lord of hosts. ‘And in this place I will give peace,’ says the Lord of hosts."

Haggai 2:9 (NKJV)

"My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."

Exodus 33:14 (NKJV)

  • Pray that Harvest Olney will be the latter house where the greater glory will reside: His Shekinah presence, salvations, healings, special miracles, deliverance, and more
  • Declare it is happening NOW. Decree acceleration, provision, and completion

Wage the Good Warfare

"This charge I commit to you, son Timothy, according to the prophecies previously made concerning you, that by them you may wage the good warfare."

1 Timothy 1:18 (NKJV)

  • Pray that every prophecy spoken concerning Harvest Olney is coming to pass now
  • Declare there is expansion, exposure, and explosion for Harvest Olney
  • Declare that Harvest Olney is a center of healing

A Great and Effective Door

Covered in the Air and on the Ground

Not Against Flesh and Blood

"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age, against spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places."

Ephesians 6:12 (NKJV)

The Harvest Olney building project

  • Bind every opposition, obstacle, hindrance, and delay to the building project
  • Decree acceleration, provision, and completion. This enterprise is blessed

"But those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles, they shall run and not be weary, they shall walk and not faint."

Isaiah 40:31 (NKJV)

  • Pray that the Lord will continue to strengthen them with might in their inner man, and with fresh fire
  • Pray that they will not grow weary but keep pressing toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus (Philippians 3:14)
To the Nations

All Authority Has Been Given to Me

"And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, ‘All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.’"

Matthew 28:18 (NKJV)

  • Pray that there will be an acceleration of churches being planted in the nations of the earth
  • Pray that those missionaries called to Europe, Asia, and Latin America will arise and go forth in the name of the Lord

Go and Make Disciples

"Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

Matthew 28:19-20 (NKJV)

  • Pray that there will be strong manifestations of God's power as these missionaries go forth
  • Pray that the resistance to the Gospel will be broken and every veil over the eyes of the people removed
  • Pray that there will be radical transformation of lives as these missionaries go forth and preach the Gospel of grace

My God Shall Supply All Your Need

"And my God shall supply all your need according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus."

Philippians 4:19 (NKJV)

  • Pray that there is a greater flow of finances for the Gospel to reach the nations
  • Pray that the Lord is raising Kingdom financiers throughout HIM-U to see the acceleration of churches, orphanages, universities, clinics, schools, and hospitals being built
  • Pray that we will be excellent stewards of the financial resources the Lord has blessed us with

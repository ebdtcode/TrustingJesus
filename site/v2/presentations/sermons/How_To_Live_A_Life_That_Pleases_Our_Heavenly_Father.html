Pleasing the Father

How To Live A Life That Pleases Our Heavenly Father

“Your life does not belong to you. Your life belongs to Jesus.”

Pastor Casimir Bilong

Ephesians 2:1-10 • Ephesians 4:31-32 • John 4:28-39 • September 2026

Nothing But an A

I grew up in Cameroon, and for the first two decades of my life my parents had a very strict rule: the only thing they would accept on our report card was an A. B was okay. C was completely unacceptable. In eighth grade I had to forge my report card because there was a C.

I always longed to hear my parents say, “Good job. I'm proud of you.” When I received my doctorate, I sent my father a message: “I'm a doctor now.” He said, “I told you a master's was not enough. At last. Now I'm proud of you.”

If you're a parent and you don't have the habit of telling your children you're proud of them, please do so. When you return home today, let your son or daughter know.

The Longing Comes From God

Sometimes we get into adulthood still trying to get our parents' validation, hoping for that phone call that says, “I'm proud of you.” Who here can relate?

Longing for your father's validation is natural, because it comes from God. God is our Heavenly Father. He created us, and He has standards of excellence: a way He wants us to live so that we can hear Him say, “I am proud of you. You did a good job.”

When we become adults, the voice that matters most is no longer our earthly parents'. It is the Heavenly Father's. So the question is: what should I do so that God says, “I am proud of you”?

Once You Were Dead

"Once you were dead because of your disobedience and your many sins. You used to live in sin, just like the rest of the world, obeying the devil, the commander of the powers in the unseen world... All of us used to live that way, following the passionate desires and inclinations of our sinful nature. By our very nature we were subject to God's anger, just like everyone else."

Ephesians 2:1-3 (NLT)

If you refuse to obey God, the spirit at work in your heart is the devil.

But God

"But God is so rich in mercy, and He loved us so much, that even though we were dead because of our sins, He gave us life when He raised Christ from the dead. It is only by God's grace that you have been saved. For He raised us from the dead along with Christ and seated us with Him in the heavenly realms because we are united with Christ Jesus. So God can point to us in all future ages as examples of the incredible wealth of His grace and kindness toward us."

Ephesians 2:4-7 (NLT)

God's Masterpiece

"God saved you by His grace when you believed. And you can't take credit for this; it is a gift from God. Salvation is not a reward for the good things we have done, so none of us can boast about it. For we are God's masterpiece. He has created us anew in Christ Jesus, so we can do the good things He planned for us long ago."

Ephesians 2:8-10 (NLT)

Three Moves

Acknowledge that your life belongs to Jesus. Embrace your new identity. Do the good works you were created for.

Part 1

Your Life Belongs to Jesus

You were dead. You did not produce the life you have.

“My Life Is Mine”

We tend to live as we see fit, giving everyone the impression that we are in control: we decide where to go, what to do, which direction. We determine our own purpose. We have our projects.

From civil rights to the Me Too movement, we have fought for personal rights that no government should take, and that's okay. But that doesn't mean our life belongs to us.

To prove it, remember the spiritual state where we used to be outside of Christ. And if you are still in that state, make things right with God this morning.

Outside of Christ

Regardless of your current life accomplishment, you are dead. This doesn't look like freedom. It is slavery.

Spiritually

Stillborn. Born dead in disobedience because of Adam's sin.

Sociologically

Of this world, following its patterns, controlled by the devil.

Psychologically

A prisoner of your sinful desires, whatever you think you are achieving.

Eschatologically

Under the wrath of God. If you die now, eternal loss.

We Were All Stillborn

This passage is in the past tense. But if you don't have a relationship with God right now, you are currently dead because of your disobedience and your many sins. You did not choose that state. It is yours by virtue of being born.

You may be doing good things. You bought your house. You have your business. You believe your life is under control. I'm here to tell you: regardless of your accomplishments, you are dead.

You are a slave of the devil as long as you are not in Christ. Amen?

Say “But God”

"But God, who is rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved)."

Ephesians 2:4-5 (NKJV)

God Gave Me Life

A day came when you surrendered your life to Christ. The life that belonged to the devil, God replaced with the life of Christ. The very power that raised Christ from the dead lives in you.

Can You Claim It as Your Own?

You didn't create this life. You were lifeless. You didn't produce it. You did nothing. The only thing you did, as Pastor Peabody preached this morning on righteousness, is believe in God.

Because of your faith, righteousness was credited to you. Salvation was credited to you. Life was infused into you.

Now, sociologically, you are no longer of this world: “I am seated with Christ in heavenly realms.” Psychologically, you are no longer a slave to sin: you can take control of your flesh. And you are no longer under wrath, but assured of eternal life with Christ.

Free Indeed, Within Boundaries

"Therefore if the Son makes you free, you shall be free indeed."

John 8:36 (NKJV)

“Indeed” means there is no other way to receive freedom. Any freedom you believe you have outside of Christ does not exist.

Does that mean I am free to do whatever I want? No. You're not free to do whatever you want. You're free to do whatever Jesus Christ wants. That's true freedom. Your life is lived within the boundaries of what Jesus wants for you.

The Alignment Question

The question should be: What does the Son want me to do? How does the Son want me to live? When you go through that quest, it pleases your Father.

God is asking us today: did you ask whether what you're doing is in alignment with what Jesus wants for your life? I cannot answer that for you. If it's not aligned, go back to God and get back on track.

Your job, your business, your marriage, whatever is going on right now: make sure it is lived within the confines of what Jesus wants for you. Spend the rest of your life fulfilling what Jesus wants, not what you want for yourself.

Part 2

Embrace Your New Identity

United with Christ, set apart on purpose

“I Am United With Christ Jesus”

"And raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus."

Ephesians 2:6 (NKJV)

Why the Righteous Get Up

Pastor P said this morning that the righteous may fall seven times but get up seven times. Why? Because you are united with Christ. That's your new identity. You can't live like a regular, dead person. You are alive in Christ, and your life must reflect it.

Examples God Can Boast About

Why are we united to Christ? Verse 7: so that God can point to us in all future ages as examples of the incredible wealth of His grace and kindness. So ask yourself: am I an example?

Like your dad with his friends: “Have you seen my daughter? She came home with all A's.” When the devil came before God, God asked him, “Have you seen My servant Job?”

Is it possible for God to be talking to the devil and say, “Have you seen My servant Bilong?” Add your name. Am I living in a way that God can boast about me?

Holiness Is Intentional

"For this is the will of God, your sanctification: that you should abstain from sexual immorality."

1 Thessalonians 4:3 (NKJV)

God made us holy by coming to live in us. We are already set apart. But practical holiness is an intentional decision we make on a daily basis: to set ourselves apart from the world, every single day.

The desires are still there, because we still have this body. But we have the power, because of Christ in us, to overcome them. Holiness is way beyond sexual sin: it is living in a way that sets you apart from the world.

Flee Is an Action Verb

We don't have the excuse “we can't help it.” Christ lives in us. Sin is no longer over us.

“Flee sexual immorality.”

1 Corinthians 6:18

“My beloved, flee from idolatry.”

1 Corinthians 10:14

“Flee also youthful lusts.”

2 Timothy 2:22

Running, fleeing: something you have the ability to do. Can we fall? Yes. Then quickly stand up and keep walking in holiness.

“I See It, but I Refuse”

Fleeing sin is a daily decision, because we will meet sin everywhere. You just need to wake up in the morning. We live in a corrupted world the devil controls, and one way he tries to control our life is to make sure sin is everywhere.

Sometimes you just want to walk with your eyes closed. You cannot. So you decide: “Yes, I see that temptation. I see it very well. But I refuse. I'm not going to fall.”

At work, in your family, sometimes even in church. Driving your car and a billboard reminds you of sin. TV, radio, internet. You have to make the intentional decision: “I will not allow this sin to take me away from the will of God for my life.”

There Is Always a Way Out

"No temptation has overtaken you except such as is common to man; but God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation will also make the way of escape, that you may be able to bear it."

1 Corinthians 10:13 (NKJV)

Remember

Christ lives in you. You are what? United with Christ. That's your identity.

Renew Your Mind, Nurture Your Spirit

Only the Word of God will renew our mind. Romans says do not conform to the patterns of this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind. It is a decision we make, because the Word is available. There should not be a single day where we don't dive into the Word.

As we renew our mind, we are armed and ready to say no to sin when it comes our way.

At the men's prayer breakfast, Deacon Hannibal asked: we are body, soul, and spirit. We spend so much time nurturing our body and soul. How much time do we spend nurturing our spirit? That's where the true battle is.

Part 3

Do the Good Works You Were Created For

A masterpiece is not lived just anyhow

You Are God's Piece of Art

"For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand that we should walk in them."

Ephesians 2:10 (NKJV)

When God saved you, He took His time to mold you into the person you are now, united with Christ. Nobody here can afford an original da Vinci, because of the time he took and the value attached to it.

Imagine God doing that work with you, giving you every feature and every skill, and you want to live just any life? You are extremely valuable to God. Too valuable to live just anyhow.

Be Kind to One Another

"Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, and evil speaking be put away from you, with all malice. And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God in Christ forgave you."

Ephesians 4:31-32 (NKJV)

The A-Plus

Because of what Christ did for you, this is the standard. Bishop said of a member who passed away that his very life preached a sermon, because he was kind to everybody. That's the kind of life that gets the Father to say, “I'm proud of you.”

Tell Your Story

"The woman then left her waterpot, went her way into the city, and said to the men, 'Come, see a Man who told me all things that I ever did.'... And many of the Samaritans of that city believed in Him because of the word of the woman."

John 4:28-29, 39 (NKJV)

We have the duty to tell the world who we are and what God has done for us. That's an assignment. You know your story. You know where Jesus took you from. Tell your story to the world.

The blessings are certain; we don't need to fight for them. But we can enjoy so many blessings in Christ and let the world perish around us. We shouldn't let that happen.

“Come, Good and Faithful Servant”

Ultimately we want to say with Paul, “I fought the good fight, I finished the race, I kept the faith,” and receive the crown of glory (2 Timothy 4:7-8).

1. Acknowledge that your life belongs to God

Express your freedom within the confines of what Jesus wants.

2. Choose to live a holy life

Pursue holiness on a daily basis.

3. Exemplify good works every single day

Be kind to others and share your story so others escape the wrath of God.

Come Forward

God Is Here

Maybe you're here today and you don't know God. This whole idea of pleasing the Father doesn't apply to you yet, because you're not a child in the first place. If you want to be a child of God, come forward.

If you're struggling with holiness, you've tried and tried, but it seems you're falling all the time, you need to be strengthened this morning. Come forward as well. And whatever burden you're carrying, come, and we will agree with you in prayer.

Prayer Points

  • Father, thank You that the longing for Your approval comes from You; let the voice I live for be Yours, saying “I am proud of you” (Matthew 25:21)
  • I acknowledge that my life does not belong to me; I was dead in my trespasses and You made me alive, so my life is Yours (Ephesians 2:1-5)

  • I embrace my identity: I am united with Christ and seated with Him in heavenly realms; let me never live like a dead person again (Ephesians 2:6; Colossians 3:1-3)

Prayer Points

  • Make my life an example You can point to and boast about in the ages to come (Ephesians 2:7; Job 1:8)
  • Give me the intentional daily decision to pursue holiness and set myself apart from the world (1 Thessalonians 4:3-5; 1 Peter 1:15-16)
  • When sin is everywhere I look, help me say “I see it, but I refuse,” and show me the way out You promised (1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Timothy 2:22)
  • Give me hunger for Your Word every single day, so my mind is renewed and my spirit nurtured for the battle (Romans 12:2; Matthew 4:4)
  • Remove bitterness, rage, anger, harsh words, and slander from me; make me kind and forgiving, and give me boldness to tell my story (Ephesians 4:31-32; John 4:39)
Declaration

My Life Belongs to Jesus

Father, my life does not belong to me. I was dead in my disobedience and my many sins, but You, rich in mercy, gave me the life of Christ. I did not produce this life; I received it by grace through faith, so I surrender it to the One who owns it. I am united with Christ Jesus. I am seated with Him in heavenly realms. I am no longer a slave to sin, and I am no longer under wrath. I choose holiness today, on purpose. I will flee sin, renew my mind in Your Word, and nurture my spirit for the battle. I am Your masterpiece, created for good works You planned long ago, so I will be kind, I will forgive, and I will tell my story. I live to hear You say, “I am proud of you.” In Jesus' name, Amen.

Study Notes

Sermon Summary

How To Live A Life That Pleases Our Heavenly Father - Pastor Casimir Bilong

Key points, principles, and takeaways

Overview

The Central Message - Preached at the second service after Pastor Peabody's message on the righteous, and at the close of Bishop Johnson's five-week series on sin. Pastor Bilong opens with his Cameroonian upbringing, where only an A was acceptable on a report card, and the lifelong longing to hear a parent say “I'm proud of you.” That longing is natural because it comes from God: the Heavenly Father has standards of excellence and a way He wants us to live so He can say, “I am proud of you. You did a good job.” The message answers one question: what should I do so that God says He is proud of me?

The Answer From Ephesians 2:1-10 - First, acknowledge that your life belongs to Jesus. You were stillborn spiritually, dead in disobedience, of this world, a prisoner of your desires, and under wrath, until “but God” gave you the life of Christ by grace through faith. You did not produce that life, so you cannot own it, and your freedom is bounded by what Jesus wants. Second, embrace your new identity: united with Christ, seated with Him in heavenly realms, and placed there so God can point to you as an example of His grace. That requires an intentional daily pursuit of holiness, fleeing sin, and renewing the mind with the Word. Third, do the good works you were created for as God's masterpiece: be kind to one another and tell your story, so that at the end you hear, “Come, good and faithful servant.”

That is slavery, not freedom.

Embrace Your Identity - United with Christ. This is why the righteous get up after falling seven times. Do not live as a regular, dead person; live a life that reflects your unity with Christ.

God Wants to Boast About You - Verse 7: united with Christ so God can point to us as examples of His grace and kindness. “Have you seen My servant...?” Add your name.

Summary 4 of 6

Principles and Warnings

Approval is found in the Father's voice - not in accomplishments.

Life received as a gift cannot be claimed as a possession - you did not produce it, so you do not own it.

Freedom in Christ is freedom to do His will - not freedom from His will.

Position precedes practice - God made you holy; now live holy, intentionally and daily.

Flee is an action verb - holiness is a decision, not a feeling.

Only the Word renews the mind - neglect of the Word is neglect of the real battlefield.

Value determines conduct - a masterpiece is not lived “just anyhow.”

Grace received is a story to be told - the blessings are certain; the assignment is to share them.

Warning: Ephesians 2:1-3 is present tense for the unsaved - you are dead regardless of house, business, or achievements, and if you die now you face eternal loss.

Warning: “my life is mine” is slavery dressed as freedom - the devil is the spirit at work in the heart that refuses to obey God.

Warning: “I can't help it” is no longer an excuse - sin is no longer over us. There is no way the Father can be proud of you if you accept sin as a fact of life and keep dwelling in it.

Warning: do not collect blessings while the world perishes - and do not skip a single day in the Word; an unrenewed mind is an unarmed mind.

Summary 5 of 6

Takeaways and Declarations

Tell your children today - that you are proud of them.

Run the alignment audit - job, business, marriage, plans: are they what Jesus wants? Realign where needed.

Confess your identity daily - “I am united with Christ. I am seated with Christ in heavenly realms. I am God's piece of art.”

Decide each morning to flee sin - “I see it, but I refuse. I'm not going to fall.”

Feed your spirit - read and meditate on the Word every single day, more than you feed body and soul.

Get up quickly - when you fall, stand and keep walking in holiness.

Be kind - put away bitterness, rage, anger, harsh words, and slander; forgive as Christ forgave you.

Tell your story this week - as the Samaritan woman did, so someone else can escape the wrath of God.

Come forward - if you do not know God, become a child of God; if you keep falling, come to be strengthened; bring every burden.

Declarations - “But God.” “God gave me life.” “I am seated with Christ in heavenly realms.” “I am united with Christ Jesus.” “I am God's piece of art.” God wants to say of you, “Have you seen My servant?” and at the end, “Come, good and faithful servant. Come and receive your crown of glory.”

Overcoming The Flesh By The Spirit

Bishop Darlingston Johnson

Romans 8:12-14

Why We Preach Against Sin

Continuing the Series on Sins We Tolerate

"The purpose of God is not to bring conviction by itself or to make you feel sorry for your sins. The purpose of God is to deliver you completely, not just from the penalty of sin, but from the power of sin."

"Christ came so that we can have power over sin. Sin does not have to rule over us and master us."

The Principal Text

"Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh. For if you live according to the flesh you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God."

Romans 8:12-14 (NKJV)

Part 1

The Struggle

Paul's battle in Romans 7 is the battle some of you are fighting now

Frustrated and Defeated

"For the good that I will to do, I do not do; but the evil I will not to do, that I practice."

Romans 7:19 (NKJV)

Paul Wanted to Do Right

He desired to obey the law but found himself not obeying it. The things he didn't want to do, he found himself doing. "He described his struggle with sin that left him frustrated and defeated, because he so much wanted to do the will of God."

Zeal Without Knowledge

  • Paul was very zealous for the things of God. "But you can be zealous and lack knowledge."
  • "If I wanted to go to Atlanta, I can be very zealous about going to Atlanta. Ninety miles an hour, I'm breaking the speed limit, but hey, I'm going the wrong way."
  • Many zealous Christians who desire to please God are frustrated because they find themselves dominated by sin.
  • The zeal must be directed and guided by knowledge so it takes you to the right place and produces the right experience.

Who Will Deliver Me?

"O wretched man that I am! Who will deliver me from this body of death? I thank God, through Jesus Christ our Lord!"

Romans 7:24-25 (NKJV)

Deliverance Is Not More Rules

Paul realizes his solution requires someone other than himself. "He's a captive and he cannot set himself free. He's tried the law, he's tried the rules, and he has remained in bondage." Then he discovers the person: Jesus Christ.

A Power Greater Than Sin

  • This body is not yet redeemed. Its desires are real and strong, and "in your own strength you are not able to overcome the force of the sin that is in your members."
  • "But there is one stronger than sin." That power is resident in a person called Jesus Christ and is found through faith in Him.
  • Gravity always brings you down. But the law of aerodynamics let the Bishop fly for 17 hours. "There was another power that had overcome the law of gravity and enabled me to do what I couldn't do by myself."

The Law of the Spirit of Life

"For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has made me free from the law of sin and death."

Romans 8:2 (NKJV)

Sin Is the Exception, Not the Rule

"The law of sin and death is there, but there's a power that is greater than that, that the believer taps into through salvation." You're not perfect, and until the redeemed body comes you will sometimes yield. "But sin would be the exception and not the rule."

The Car in the Ditch

"He who raised Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who dwells in you."

Romans 8:11 (NKJV)

  • You push, but the car in the ditch won't move. Then a strong man shows up: "Sister, let me help you." What you could never do, he does with ease.
  • "He doesn't just stand with you, He's in you." The Helper supplies the strength and power your body lacks to overcome the sin that is overcoming it.
Part 2

Know Where You Are

"You are not in the flesh but in the Spirit" (Romans 8:8-9)

In the Flesh, You Cannot Please God

"So then, those who are in the flesh cannot please God."

Romans 8:8 (NKJV)

  • To be in the flesh means you are in Adam, not saved. You can be religious and still be in the flesh.
  • The religious person becomes either frustrated, trying so hard and failing, or proud, deceived into thinking he is doing it.
  • The rich young ruler kept the commandments from his youth, yet he had an idol he desired more than Christ. "No matter how sincere you are, you cannot."

"But You"

"But you are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not His."

Romans 8:9 (NKJV)

Positionally, Always in the Spirit

"You know you're in the Spirit because of this truth: the Spirit of God dwells in you. Not because of your performance." If the Spirit is in you, that is proof you belong to Christ.

Stop Trying To Get In The Spirit

  • "You don't need to get in the Spirit. God did that for you. Jesus did that for you."
  • "Tell your neighbor, stop trying to get in the Spirit. That's part of your problem."
  • "All the energy and time you're spending trying to get in the Spirit is one of the things the enemy is using to exhaust you, tire you out, and make you want to quit."
  • Start living and acting like someone who is in the Spirit and in whom the Spirit lives.
Part 3

Debtors No Longer

"Therefore" is there for a reason (Romans 8:12-14)

No Longer Obligated

  • Because you are in Christ, in the Spirit, with the Spirit in you, you are "no longer obligated to, forced to, live in sin."
  • "You don't have to continue to commit fornication. You don't have to continue to engage in pornography. You don't have to continue to be unforgiving."
  • "You can literally say no and walk away from it. Isn't that good news?"
  • "There's no excuse for a believer not to be living a holy life," because Christ has made provision for it.

Live or Die

"For if you live according to the flesh you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live."

Romans 8:13 (NKJV)

Habitually

Those who habitually obey the dictates and desires of the flesh will perish, and "that is talking about eternal damnation." Those who habitually put to death the sinful deeds of the body will live, and that is everlasting life. "So now, that is very serious."

Is This Salvation by Works?

No. You are saved by grace alone through faith alone.

If salvation depended on your performance, "that would put you right back under the law, which would make the cross of Christ of no effect in your life."

What it teaches: make your calling and election sure.

"It is not you putting to death the deeds of the body that saves you, but if you are saved, you will be putting to death the deeds of the body." You will know them by their fruits.

Waging War or Making Peace?

"For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God."

Romans 8:14 (NKJV)

The Test of the Spirit's Presence

"Am I waging war against sin, or have I made peace with sin?" Every son of God in whom the Spirit lives will be led by the Spirit to put sin to death. "It doesn't mean you are doing a great job at it, but you will be led by the Spirit." Making peace with sin shows the Spirit is not working in you.

The Bottom Line

If the Spirit of God lives in you, you are a child of God.
If you are a child of God, the Spirit of God will lead you.
If the Spirit is leading you, He will always lead you to wage war against sin.

"How you are treating sin is one of the evidences that demonstrates the reality of your salvation."

His Primary Ministry

  • "Many of you think the primary function of the Holy Spirit is to help you fulfill your dream": a vision, a ministry, a business, a position, a talent, "and make me great."
  • "No. His primary ministry is to make you like Christ." He has come to give you victory over sin and to conform you to Christ.
  • That means victory not only over the penalty of sin but over the power of sin. "Sin should be an exception, not the rule of your life."
Part 4

By the Spirit

You are the one who must put sin to death, but there is only one way to do it

What the Law Could Not Do

"For what the law could not do in that it was weak through the flesh, God did by sending His own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, on account of sin: He condemned sin in the flesh."

Romans 8:3 (NKJV)

Old Covenant and New

The law could show you your sin and condemn you, but "the law could not give you power not to sin." Christ satisfied the law's demands, so God declares you righteous, places the Spirit inside you, and the Spirit gives you the power you didn't have.

Only One Way

"'Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit,' says the LORD of hosts."

Zechariah 4:6 (NKJV)

  • "You cannot do it by willpower, so stop trying to. You cannot do it by your own effort, so stop trying to."
  • "Don't make the mistake the next time you're tempted to immediately try to do it in your strength." Realize you cannot, and solicit the help of the Holy Spirit.
  • If victory is by the Spirit, "I need to know the Spirit. I need to understand how the Spirit works."

The Helper Jesus Promised

"And I will pray the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may abide with you forever... He will glorify Me, for He will take of what is Mine and declare it to you."

John 14:16; John 16:14 (NKJV)

Not Left to Do It Alone

"I'm not going to tell you to do it and then leave it to you to do. I'm going to tell you to do it, and then I am going to, through the Holy Spirit, give you the help, the power, the ability you need."

How the Spirit Helps You

1. He reveals Jesus to you in the Word.

"If you want to be victorious over sin, see Jesus." In His goodness, His mercy, His salvation.

2. He reveals who you are in Christ.

The clearer your spiritual identity, the stronger you are and the easier it is to resist.

3. He puts the Word in your heart and in your mouth.

The Word is the sword of the Spirit. "Notice all of this requires your cooperation."

Fill the Vessel

  • "When you are being tempted, or even before you are tempted, don't try to deal with the sin directly. Instead, cultivate your relationship with the Spirit."
  • An empty bottle is full of air. You can try to suck the air out with a vacuum, or you can displace it by pouring in good clean water.
  • "You're not trying to extract sin. You're filling your vessel" with the things of the Spirit, beginning with the Spirit showing you Jesus in the Word.
  • "Keep allowing the Spirit to show you Jesus, and the stronger you will become, and the easier it would be to overcome sin."

Your Identity Is the Foundation

"If You are the Son of God, command that these stones become bread."

Matthew 4:3 (NKJV)

  • The devil's main strategy is to make you doubt your identity in Christ. He tried it on Jesus: "If he had gotten Jesus to doubt who He was, he would have weakened Jesus sufficiently."
  • You are a son of God. You are not in the flesh but in the Spirit. The Spirit of God lives in you. You are the righteousness of God in Christ. "That's your identity."

The Sword of the Spirit

"And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God."

Ephesians 6:17 (NKJV)

Jesus Spoke the Word

When Jesus was tempted, He took the Word the Spirit had placed in His heart and spoke it, "and when He spoke that Word, the power connected to that Word was released, and He was delivered." Let the Spirit put the Word in your heart and your mouth, so that is what comes out when temptation comes.

Part 5

Practical Steps

"I'm trying to be practical here"

Practice His Presence

"The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all."

2 Corinthians 13:14 (NKJV)

  • "He's in you whether you feel it or not." Cultivate a mental consciousness of His presence throughout the day. Talk to Him, ask Him questions, listen for answers.
  • "He usually sounds like your own voice." He puts thoughts in your mind and opens your eyes to see things.
  • "He's your best friend, your closest friend, your wisest friend." He is not just a force. He is a real person.

When You Do Sin

  • On the occasion that you didn't draw the strength He was ready to give, "remember that the fellowship with Him is not broken. Why? Because your sins are all forgiven."
  • "He's not going to withdraw." Sin may hinder you from hearing Him and fellowshipping with Him.
  • Quickly acknowledge what you've done, thank God that the blood of Jesus has taken it away, and engage the Holy Spirit again.
  • "So the next time you face that temptation, this time you will have the strength and power that He gives you to walk away from it."

Pray in Advance

"And do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

Matthew 6:13 (NKJV)

Every Day, Before the Day Starts

"Holy Spirit, as I begin this day, I'm going to be depending upon You for the strength and the power not to yield to temptation." If somebody at the office is your temptation, pray before you walk in: "Temptation is waiting for me in there, but I thank You I don't have to yield."

Pray in the Moment

When the temptation begins to manifest, pray again: "Lord, right now I am being tempted. I don't have the strength in myself, but I thank You, Holy Spirit, for helping me now." One of two things may happen.

He infuses you with His presence.

"That temptation simply loses its appeal. It's like it's not even happening."

He gives you an instruction.

"Right now you need to get up and go for a walk." "Go home and take a cold shower." Ask Him to help you take the first step, and start taking it.

Starve the Flesh

  • "One of the ways you put to death the desires of the flesh is not to feed it. You feed your spirit, so your spirit keeps growing, but starve those desires."
  • "If you have a problem with pornography, you need to start starving it." Disconnect the cable. Block those pages. When you get in bed, cut off the phone completely.
  • "Starve the desire, but don't depend upon that." Depend upon the Holy Spirit for the power that comes from within.

Make Your Calling and Election Sure

"The first invitation is to the man who has religion but is not in Christ"

If you have made peace with sin, come and let someone pray with you. If you are fighting a spiritual battle, personal or in your family, come and let God's people join you. Physical, health, and financial needs are all part of the salvation package. "My God shall supply all of your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus."

Study Notes

Sermon Summary

Overcoming The Flesh By The Spirit - Bishop Darlingston Johnson

Key points, principles, and takeaways

Overview

Continuing the series on sins the church must not tolerate, Bishop Johnson turns from diagnosis to deliverance. Conviction is not the goal; "the purpose of God is to deliver you completely, not just from the penalty of sin, but from the power of sin." From Romans 7 and 8 he shows that the believer is no longer a debtor to the flesh, and that victory is appropriated by the Holy Spirit alone, never by willpower, law-keeping, or self-effort.

The struggle - Paul wanted to do right and could not. He tried the law and the rules and remained in bondage, until he discovered that deliverance is a person: "I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord."

The position - Every believer is already in the Spirit because the Spirit dwells in him. Stop trying to get in the Spirit and start living like someone who is.

The practice - Put the deeds of the body to death by the Spirit: let Him show you Jesus and your identity in the Word, put the Word in your mouth, practice His presence, pray in advance and in the moment, and starve the flesh.

Context - Preached at Harvest Intercontinental Church, Olney, with Pastor Kofi, Elder Frange, and missionary evangelist Antoinette, home from Rwanda, in attendance.

Summary 1 of 10

Key Scriptures (1)

Romans 7:15, 18-19 - "For the good that I will to do, I do not do; but the evil I will not to do, that I practice." Paul's frustration describes the zealous believer who wants to obey and keeps failing by sheer will.

Romans 7:24-25 - "O wretched man that I am! Who will deliver me from this body of death? I thank God, through Jesus Christ our Lord!" Read aloud together. The answer to the struggle is not more rules but a person.

Romans 8:2 - "The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has made me free from the law of sin and death." The power greater than sin, like aerodynamics overcoming gravity without removing it.

Romans 8:3 - "What the law could not do in that it was weak through the flesh, God did." The law could expose and condemn sin; it could never justify or empower. Christ did both.

Romans 8:8 - "Those who are in the flesh cannot please God." In the flesh means in Adam, unsaved. No sincerity, zeal, or performance from that position pleases God.

Matthew 19:16-22 - The rich young ruler kept the commandments from his youth, yet an idol held first place in his heart. Religious self-deception.

Romans 8:9 - "You are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you." Positionally, the believer is always in the Spirit, by salvation and not by performance.

Summary 2 of 10

Key Scriptures (2)

Romans 8:11 - "He who raised Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who dwells in you." The Spirit quickens the body with strength it lacks, like a strong man lifting a car out of a ditch.

Romans 8:12-14 - "We are debtors, not to the flesh... if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God." The core text: no longer obligated to sin, and mortifying sin is the evidence, not the price, of salvation.

Matthew 7:20; 2 Peter 1:10 - "By their fruits you will know them." "Make your calling and election sure." Your attitude toward sin is the fruit that confirms the Spirit is in you.

John 14:16; 16:14 - "He will give you another Helper... He will take of what is Mine and declare it to you." The Spirit's first way of helping is to reveal Jesus in the Scriptures.

Matthew 4:3, 6 - "If You are the Son of God..." The devil's strategy against Jesus, and against you, is to make you doubt your identity.

Ephesians 6:17 - "The sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God." The Spirit's weapon of choice; Jesus defeated temptation by speaking it.

2 Corinthians 13:14 - "The communion of the Holy Spirit." He is the person of the Godhead who loves to fellowship and talk.

Matthew 6:13; Zechariah 4:6; Philippians 4:19 - "Deliver us from the evil one." "Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit." "My God shall supply all your need." Pray in advance, win by the Spirit, and expect every need met.

Summary 3 of 10

Main Teachings (1)

Conviction is for deliverance - Sermons on unforgiveness, gossip, and lukewarmness brought conviction, but God does not stop there. Christ came so that we can have power over sin, and "sin does not have to rule over us and master us."

Zeal without knowledge - You can drive 90 miles an hour toward New York while trying to reach Atlanta. Many sincere, zealous Christians are frustrated because they lack the knowledge of what they have in Christ.

Deliverance is a person, not more rules - Paul was a captive who could not set himself free. "He's tried the law, he's tried the rules, and he has remained in bondage." His solution was Jesus Christ.

A power greater than sin - The pull of sin in the unredeemed body is real and strong, but the law of the Spirit of life is stronger, just as flight overcomes gravity. Sin becomes "the exception and not the rule."

The Helper is in you, not beside you - Jesus said you will never deal with sin alone. The Spirit quickens your mortal body, supplying the energy and power it lacks, like the strong man who lifts the car you cannot move.

Summary 4 of 10

Main Teachings (2)

In the flesh means in Adam - The unsaved cannot please God however sincere. The religious person ends up either frustrated, trying and failing, or proud and self-deceived like the rich young ruler, who kept the commandments yet worshiped an idol every day.

You are already in the Spirit - "You know you're in the Spirit because of this truth: the Spirit of God dwells in you. Not because of your performance." Positionally the believer is always in the Spirit, and the Spirit's presence is the proof you belong to Christ.

Stop trying to get in the Spirit - "God did that for you. Jesus did that for you." The energy spent trying to get what you already have is "one of the things the enemy is using to exhaust you, tire you out, and make you want to quit."

Debtors no longer - "Therefore" points back to all of this. You are no longer obligated to the flesh. You don't have to continue in fornication, pornography, or unforgiveness. "There's no excuse for a believer not to be living a holy life."

Live or perish - Those who habitually obey the flesh will perish, and that is eternal damnation. Those who habitually put the deeds of the body to death by the Spirit will live, and that is everlasting life.

Summary 5 of 10

Main Teachings (3)

Not salvation by works - Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone. Tying it to performance would "make the cross of Christ of no effect." Romans 8:13 teaches that the saved can make their calling and election sure by observing what they do with sin. Mortifying sin does not save you; if you are saved, you will be mortifying sin.

Waging war or making peace - Every son of God is led by the Spirit to put sin to death. "It doesn't mean you are doing a great job at it, but you will be led by the Spirit." If you claim the Spirit but make peace with sin, the Spirit is not leading you.

The Spirit's primary ministry - Not to fulfill your dream, ministry, business, or position and "make me great," as much of the charismatic movement assumes. "His primary ministry is to make you like Christ," giving victory over the power of sin, not only its penalty.

What the law could not do - The law showed sin and condemned it but could not justify or empower. Christ satisfied its demands, God declared you righteous, placed the Spirit in you, and the Spirit gives the power the law never could.

You put sin to death, by the Spirit - The Spirit does not do it for you, but He gives you the power. "You cannot do it by willpower, so stop trying to." Not by power, not by might, but by the Spirit.

Summary 6 of 10

How the Spirit Helps

He reveals Jesus in the Word - Jesus said the Spirit would take the things of Christ and reveal them to you. "If you want to be victorious over sin, see Jesus." Don't attack sin directly; cultivate the Spirit. You don't vacuum the air out of a bottle, you pour in water. "You're not trying to extract sin. You're filling your vessel."

He reveals who you are in Christ - The devil's main strategy is to make you doubt your identity, as he did with Jesus: "If You are the Son of God." You are a son of God, not in the flesh but in the Spirit, indwelt by the Spirit, the righteousness of God in Christ. Be reminded of it every day.

He puts the Word in your heart and mouth - The Word is the sword of the Spirit. Jesus spoke the Word the Spirit had placed in His heart, "and when He spoke that Word, the power connected to that Word was released." All of this requires your cooperation: prioritize time in the Word with the Spirit.

Practice His presence - He is in you whether you feel it or not. Talk to Him, ask Him questions, listen. "He usually sounds like your own voice," putting thoughts in your mind and opening your eyes. He is your best, closest, and wisest friend, a real person and not a force, and the one in the Godhead who loves to fellowship.

Summary 7 of 10

Principles

Conviction is for deliverance, not condemnation - God convicts in order to free you from the power of sin, not to make you feel sorry.

Zeal must be guided by knowledge - Energy without direction produces frustration and can carry you the wrong way entirely.

Deliverance is a person, not a rule - Law can expose and condemn sin; only Christ by the Spirit can empower obedience.

Position precedes practice - You are in the Spirit by salvation, not by striving. Resting in that identity is the foundation of victory.

Salvation produces war against sin - It never produces peace with sin. Fruit confirms salvation; it does not earn it.

Sin is the exception, not the rule - The new covenant believer still faces temptation but is no longer mastered by it.

Fill, don't extract - The way to displace sin is to be filled with the Spirit and the Word.

The Word is the Spirit's weapon - Victory is tied to how much Word is in your heart and ready in your mouth.

Forgiven sin hinders fellowship but does not sever it - He does not withdraw. Acknowledge quickly and re-engage.

Summary 8 of 10

Warnings and Cautions

Against zeal without knowledge - Sincere, hard-working believers can be driving fast in the wrong direction.

Against willpower and self-effort - "You cannot do it by your own effort, so stop trying to." Trying in your own strength when tempted is the mistake to avoid.

Against religious self-deception - Like the rich young ruler, you can keep the rules and still have an idol in first place. The religious person is either proud or condemned.

Against making peace with sin - If you claim the Spirit lives in you but you have made peace with sin, that is a reason to ask whether you have truly received Him. Those who habitually live according to the flesh will perish.

Against doubting your identity - The devil's "If you are the Son of God" is aimed at you too. Doubt weakens you enough to yield.

Against a dream-centered view of the Spirit - His primary work is to conform you to Christ, not to make you great.

Against treating sin casually - Fellowship is not broken, but sin hinders your hearing. Do not let it linger.

Against trusting practical steps alone - Starve the desire, "but don't depend upon that." Depend on the Spirit.

Summary 9 of 10

Takeaways and Declarations

Takeaways - Stop trying to get in the Spirit and start living like you are. Ask the Spirit daily to show you Jesus and yourself in Christ. Store the Word so it is in your mouth when temptation comes. Practice His presence all day. Pray in advance each morning and before known temptations. Pray again in the moment and expect either His presence or an instruction, such as a walk or a cold shower. Starve the flesh: disconnect the cable, block the pages, cut off the phone at night. When you fall, acknowledge quickly, thank God for the blood, and re-engage.

Confessions led from the pulpit - "I thank God, through Jesus Christ I am in the Spirit, the Spirit is in me, and I belong to Christ." "Lord, I thank You that I am not a debtor to sin. I am free in Christ to obey You and to please God." "Holy Spirit, you are my best friend, my closest friend, my wisest friend."

Prayer over the people - That the present-day ministry and power of the Holy Spirit will become more real to every believer. The power of sexual immorality and pornography operating freely in God's children is broken by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Declarations - "It is not that they will no longer be tempted, but this time they will not yield. This time they will resist. This time they will overcome." "We shall be a people who are waging war against sin and not making peace with it." "My God shall supply all of your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus."

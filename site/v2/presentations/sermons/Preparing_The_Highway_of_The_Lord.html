Preparing The Highway of The Lord

Pastor Maima Moore

Isaiah 40:3-5

The Principal Text

"The voice of one crying in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight in the desert a highway for our God. Every valley shall be exalted and every mountain and hill brought low; the crooked places shall be made straight and the rough places smooth; the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see it together; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken.'"

Isaiah 40:3-5 (NKJV)

Many Voices, One Voice

  • "There are many voices that are clamoring for our attention." The voice of politics. The voice of the economy. Distractions, here and there.
  • "The Holy Ghost is telling us that there is the voice of one that we should be paying attention to."
  • And what is that voice saying? "Prepare the way of the Lord."

The Wilderness

A Dry Place

"The wilderness is a place of barrenness. The wilderness is a place of unfruitfulness. The wilderness is a dry place. And in the lives of many believers, and even many churches, there is a wilderness."

But there is the voice of one crying in the wilderness.

Turn Away From, Turn To

"Prepare the way of the Lord" means to turn to the Lord. Fully turn.

"Leave gossip, leave unforgiveness, come up higher. That is the voice of the Spirit of God that has been speaking to us as a people."

Road Construction

  • Valleys raised, mountains lowered, crooked places straightened, rough places smoothed. "That is road construction. That is how they construct a highway."
  • "It's a personal call to consecrate and to dedicate, to put off the old man and to put on the new man."
  • "Before He comes, He's coming in great power to the entire church."

A King Has His Way

"Lift up your heads, O you gates! And be lifted up, you everlasting doors! And the King of glory shall come in. Who is this King of glory? The Lord strong and mighty."

Psalm 24:7-8 (NKJV)

When a mayor or a president is coming, people line the roads, stop the cars, and clean up the place. "They want to welcome a majesty."

Three Highways to Prepare

1. Your personal highway

"Make a way in your life."

2. Your family highway

"Make a way in your family."

3. A corporate highway

"Make a way in your church," for the King of glory.

The Promise

The Glory Shall Be Revealed

"We won't have to tell them. They will see."

"The glory of God is Jesus Christ." And today He lives in you: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" (Colossians 1:27).

Three Dimensions of Glory

1. The nature of God

"God is love. Work on your love life." Put on peace. Forget anxiousness, worry, and fear.

2. The manifested presence

"When people enter our services, we don't have to tell them the King of glory is here. They will know."

3. The manifested power

Miracles, signs, wonders, gifts of healing. "We need all three."

Arise and Shine

"Arise, shine; for your light has come! And the glory of the Lord is risen upon you. For behold, the darkness shall cover the earth, and deep darkness the people; but the Lord will arise over you, and His glory will be seen upon you."

Isaiah 60:1-2 (NKJV)

"The world has not seen the degree of darkness that is about to come upon the earth. But God is not moved. He's unperturbed."

The Pattern

First, a Preparation

"Every time there is an outpouring, in the Bible and in history, there was a time of preparation"

Clean Your House

"Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you."

Joshua 3:5 (NKJV)

Moses told the people to wash their clothes (Exodus 19:10). "Clean your physical house. Clean your spiritual house. Clean your emotional house. Take out the garbage."

"We will see wonders beyond wonders. But we have to make the highway."

Valleys and Mountains

Valleys to be raised

"Those small thinkings, inferiority complexes, emotional hang-ups, those are valleys."

Mountains to be lowered

"It's pride. Some of us are so prideful we cannot lift up our hands in the presence of God."

"You are nothing. I am nothing. Jesus is everything."

Humble Yourself

  • "Humble yourself enough to obey God. Humble yourself enough to obey His word."
  • "There are some people He tells you to forgive, but you say, this person hurt me. Humble yourself and forgive them."
  • "Humble yourself to say I'm sorry when you really don't want to say it." It is hard on your pride, but you want to carry glory.

First the Humbling, Then the Rain

"Consecrate a fast, call a sacred assembly... Let the bridegroom go out from his chamber, and the bride from her dressing room. Let the priests, who minister to the Lord, weep between the porch and the altar."

Joel 2:15-17 (NKJV)

Then the former and the latter rain (2:23), and "I will pour out My Spirit" (2:28). "But first comes a humbling. But first comes obedience. But first comes making a way."

Elijah on Carmel

Build an Altar

Individually, as families, and then as a church

Come Near

"Then Elijah said to all the people, 'Come near to me.' So all the people came near to him. And he repaired the altar of the Lord that was broken down."

1 Kings 18:30 (NKJV)

"Idols are things that are more important to us than God. We will never say it, but our actions show" it: reputation, comfort.

"An altar is a place where you die to yourself, you die to your plans, you die to your will, you die to your agendas."

The Family Altar

Glory in the Homes

"I want family altars. I want parents that can pray together with their children. I don't just want My presence and My glory in the assembly of the saints. I want it in the homes."

"Husbands and wives, repair your relationships." As much as depends on you, live peaceably (Romans 12:18). "You do the right thing, and God will honor you for that."

Then the Fire Fell

"Then the fire of the Lord fell... Now when all the people saw it, they fell on their faces; and they said, 'The Lord, He is God! The Lord, He is God!'"

1 Kings 18:38-39 (NKJV)

"Your sacrifice needs incense. It needs prayer." Don't wait for church to pray, worship, or fast. "Turn your bedroom into an altar."

The unconvincible "will come running to you and say, I don't know what it is you have, but that your God, I want Him."

A Day of Restoration

"So I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten, the crawling locust, the consuming locust, and the chewing locust."

Joel 2:25 (NKJV)

Those stubborn children. That job you couldn't get for five years. "It's a day of restoration of everything that has been lost. Lost sons, lost coins, and lost glory."

Exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think (Ephesians 3:20).

A Liberian Parable

The Noise in the Market

"If you pay attention to the noise in the market, you will not buy your fish"

"Let us pay attention to what God is saying. Let us pay attention to what God is doing. Let us be focused."

The Birth Position

"For we do not know what we should pray for as we ought, but the Spirit Himself makes intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered."

Romans 8:26 (NKJV)

Ahab saw fire fall and went up to eat and drink. Elijah went up Carmel to travail. "Harvesters, get in the birth position. It is giving yourself to the Lord for prevailing prayer. Pray until."

"Your best prayer in your known language is limited. Pray in the Holy Ghost."

After the Fire, the Rain

"Then the hand of the Lord came upon Elijah; and he girded up his loins and ran ahead of Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel."

1 Kings 18:46 (NKJV)

"Fire to purify, to purge, and to ignite. And then rain. It's the rain that will give us the harvest."

"The supernatural ability of God will come upon our natural ability, and we will outrun all that is man-made." Not by might nor by power (Zechariah 4:6).

Response

Prayer Points

"Lord, I prepare the way"

Prayer Points (1 of 2)

  • Ears for the one voice. Ask for grace to hear the Spirit above politics, the economy, and every distraction. (Isaiah 40:3)
  • A full turn. Turn away from gossip, unforgiveness, and every idol of reputation or comfort, and turn to the Lord.
  • Valleys raised, mountains lowered. Pray against small thinking and inferiority, and ask God to bring down pride. (Isaiah 40:4)
  • A clean house. Consecrate your home, your heart, and your emotions for the wonders God will do. (Joshua 3:5)

Prayer Points (2 of 2)

  • A family altar. Pray for restored marriages and for parents and children who pray together. (Romans 12:18)
  • Fire on the altar. Ask for a life of prayer, worship, the Word, and fasting beyond your present level of comfort. (1 Kings 18:38-39)
  • Restoration. Thank God for restoring the lost years, lost sons, lost coins, and lost glory. (Joel 2:25)
  • Rain and harvest. Pray in the Spirit until the abundance of rain and the harvest of souls. (Romans 8:26, Joel 2:23, 28)
Declaration

Lord, I Prepare the Way

I prepare my life. I prepare my family. I prepare this house.
Eating and drinking while God is moving is not my portion. I will pray until.
I hear the sound of an abundance of rain. It is not by might, not by power, but by the Holy Ghost.
Study Notes

Sermon Summary

Preparing The Highway of The Lord - Pastor Maima Moore

Key points, principles, and takeaways

Overview

Pastor Maima Moore preaches from Isaiah 40:3-5 that amid the many voices clamoring for attention there is the voice of one crying in the wilderness: prepare the way of the Lord. Preparing the highway is road construction in the heart, the home, and the church, raising valleys and lowering the mountains of pride, because the King of glory is coming in great power to His church before He comes again. Elijah on Mount Carmel is the pattern: build an altar, pray until the fire falls, and stay in prevailing prayer until the rain and the harvest come.

Context - Preached at Harvest Intercontinental Church, Olney, on Sunday, November 8, 2026. It ties together the church's recent series ("Leave gossip, leave unforgiveness, come up higher") and the previous week's message that true maturity is to develop in love.

Central claim - "Every time there is an outpouring, in the Bible and in history, there was a time of preparation."

Summary 1 of 9

Key Scriptures (1)

Isaiah 40:3-5 - "The voice of one crying in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight in the desert a highway for our God.'" The principal text. Fulfilled first in John the Baptist, and now the Spirit's summons to every believer, family, and church.

Psalm 24:7-8 - "And the King of glory shall come in. Who is this King of glory? The Lord strong and mighty." The One the highway is built for.

Colossians 1:27 - "Christ in you, the hope of glory." The glory of God is Jesus Christ, and today He lives in the believer.

Matthew 4:16 - "The people who sat in darkness have seen a great light." The great light was the appearance of Jesus, and the world is still waiting to see it.

Matthew 24:37 and Genesis 6:5 - The days of Noah, when the wickedness of man was great in the earth. The darkness will deepen.

Isaiah 60:1-4 - "Arise, shine; for your light has come... kings to the brightness of your rising... your sons shall come from afar." Darkness cannot be stopped, but the glory will come upon the church and bring a "tsunami of souls."

Exodus 19:10 - "Consecrate them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their clothes." Preparation before God came down on Sinai.

Joshua 3:5 - "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you." Preparation before the Jordan parted.

Summary 2 of 9

Key Scriptures (2)

Joel 2:15-17, 23, 28 - The sacred assembly, bridegroom and bride leaving their chambers and priests at the altar, followed by the former and latter rain and "I will pour out My Spirit." Humbling comes before outpouring.

Joel 2:25 - "I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten." It is a day of restoration.

1 Kings 18:30-39 - Elijah says "Come near," repairs the altar, soaks the sacrifice, prays, and the fire falls. The people cry, "The Lord, He is God!"

1 Kings 18:41-46 - Ahab eats and drinks while Elijah bows on Carmel. "There is the sound of abundance of rain," and the hand of the Lord carries Elijah ahead of the chariot to Jezreel.

Romans 12:18 - "If it is possible, as much as depends on you, live peaceably with all men." For difficult marriages and homes.

Ephesians 5:26 - "The washing of water by the word." The water Elijah poured on the altar pictures the Word.

Ephesians 3:20 - "Exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think." The scale of what God is about to do.

Romans 8:26 - "The Spirit Himself makes intercession for us." Why prevailing prayer is prayer in the Holy Ghost.

Zechariah 4:6 - "Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit." How the church will outrun the natural.

Summary 3 of 9

Main Teachings (1)

The voice of one - Politics, the economy, and distractions all clamor, but the Holy Ghost points to one voice crying in the wilderness. The wilderness is barrenness, unfruitfulness, a dry place, and it exists in many believers and churches. The voice says: turn away from, turn to.

Road construction - Isaiah's valleys, mountains, crooked and rough places describe how a highway is built. The call is personal: consecrate, dedicate, put off the old man and put on the new, on three highways, personal, family, and corporate. A king has his way prepared; people line the roads and clean up for a mayor or a president.

Three dimensions of glory - The glory of God is Jesus Christ, now in us, and it shows as the nature of God (love, joy, peace, gentleness), the manifested presence that visitors recognize without being told, and the manifested power of miracles, signs, wonders, and healing. "We need all three."

Glory in the darkness - The world will grow darker, as in the days of Noah, and "we cannot stop it." God is unperturbed, because Isaiah 60 promises the glory will rise on His people and draw kings, sons, and daughters.

Summary 4 of 9

Main Teachings (2)

Preparation precedes outpouring - Moses at Sinai, Joshua at the Jordan, and Joel's sacred assembly all show cleansing and humbling first, then wonders, rain, and the Spirit poured out.

Valleys and mountains - Valleys are small thinking, inferiority complexes, and emotional hang-ups. Mountains and hills are pride, which shows in refusing to lift hands, to obey hard words, to forgive, or to say "I'm sorry," especially between spouses.

Build an altar - Elijah called the people near and repaired the altar. An altar is a place of sacrifice, where you die to your plans, will, and agendas, and a place of prayer. God wants altars in individuals, in families, and in the church, with parents praying with their children.

Fire, then rain - Prayer on the altar brought the fire, and the fire convinced the unconvincible. After the fire Elijah took "the birth position" in prevailing prayer until the rain. Fire purifies, purges, and ignites; rain gives the harvest.

Outrunning the chariot - The one who heard the voice outran Ahab's chariot. If we prepare the highway, "we will outrun everything that is natural."

Summary 5 of 9

Principles

Preparation comes before outpouring. Every time, in the Bible and in history.

To prepare the way is to turn. Turn away from, and turn to.

A king has his way. You make a road for the one you honor.

The glory of God is Jesus Christ. His nature, His presence, and His power, and we need all three.

Humility is the road crew. "You are nothing. I am nothing. Jesus is everything."

An idol is anything more important to you than God. Our actions show it even when we never say it.

Sacrifice needs incense. The altar is a place of dying to self and a place of prayer.

Religion is hearing the Word over and over and not changing.

After the fire is the rain. And the rain gives the harvest.

It is not by might, nor by power. God's supernatural ability comes upon our natural ability.

Summary 6 of 9

Warnings

The noise in the market - "If you pay attention to the noise in the market, you will not buy your fish." Get caught up in the confusion and you forget your purpose.

Deepening darkness - "The world has not seen the degree of darkness that is about to come upon the earth." Do not be shaken by it; arise and shine.

Pride - Too proud to lift your hands, to obey, to forgive, or to apologize.

Hidden idols - Reputation and comfort placed above God.

Quarreling homes, smiling pews - Fussing and arguing all the way to church, then putting on a praise face at the door.

Hearing without changing - "We've been hearing this series of messages. How many of us have changed?"

Ahab's response - He had just seen fire fall from heaven and went to eat and drink. "That is not my portion."

Waiting for church - Do not wait for a service, a worship team, or a corporate fast before you pray, worship, and fast.

Summary 7 of 9

Practical Takeaways

Clean your house - Physical, spiritual, and emotional. Take out the garbage, including what is on the walls of your children's bedrooms.

Work on your love life - Put on love, joy, peace, and gentleness. Put off anxiousness, worry, and fear.

Forgive and apologize - Humble yourself to say "I'm so sorry, please forgive me," even when it may be taken as vindication.

Start a family altar - Parents pray with your children. Husbands and wives, repair the relationship.

Turn your bedroom into an altar - Spend time in prayer, the Word, worship, and fasting, beyond your present level of comfort.

Ask what has changed - Measure the recent series by change, not by attendance.

Stay focused - Pay attention to what God is saying and doing, not to the noise.

Pray until - Take the birth position. Pray in the Holy Ghost, in tongues, and let Him pray His purposes through you.

Summary 8 of 9

Words Declared

A word from that morning's service - "Stop crying. I am your deliverer. I'm your restorer."

Wonders - "People of God, we will see wonders beyond wonders."

Restoration - "It's a day of restoration of everything that has been lost. Lost sons, lost coins, and lost glory. The days of restoration are upon us."

Harvest - A "tsunami of souls," and unconvinced people who "will come running to you and say, that your God, I want Him."

Rain - "I declare, I hear the sound of an abundance of rain. The early and the latter rain, the outpouring of the Holy Ghost."

Acceleration - "We will outrun all that is man-made, all that is man-inspired, all that is man's wisdom."

The congregation's response - "Lord, I prepare the way. I prepare my life. I prepare my family."

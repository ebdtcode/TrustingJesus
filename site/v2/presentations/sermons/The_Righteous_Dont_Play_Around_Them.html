Proverbs 24

The Righteous: Don't Play Around Them!

“A righteous man may fall seven times and rise again, but the wicked shall fall by calamity.”

Pastor Zangai Peabody

Proverbs 24:15-16 • Romans 5:1 • Genesis 15:6 • Romans 3:21-25 • First Service • September 2026

A People the World Doesn't Understand

"Do not lie in wait, O wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous; do not plunder his resting place; for a righteous man may fall seven times and rise again, but the wicked shall fall by calamity."

Proverbs 24:15-16 (NKJV)

Hear the Voice of the Lord

If anyone here fits that description, “O wicked man,” hear this: leave the righteous man alone. Don't mess with his resting place. Let the sleeping lion lie sleeping.

Three Versions, One Warning

NKJV

A righteous man may fall seven times and he gets back up seven times. But the wicked man falls, bata-bata, finish. He falls by calamity.

Amplified

“Lie not in wait as a wicked man against the dwelling of the uncompromisingly righteous, the one who is upright in right standing with God.”

NIV

“Do not lurk like a thief near the house of the righteous... though the righteous fall seven times, they rise again, but the wicked stumble when calamity strikes.”

Part 1

Who Is the Righteous?

Clearing up a famous misunderstanding

“There Is None Righteous”

"As it is written: 'There is none righteous, no, not one.'"

Romans 3:10 (NKJV)

Growing up as a young believer, this is the verse people always quote. You're trying to step up in faith, and they say your own Bible already said no one is righteous. So you are a liar, you are a bigot, you are “holier than thou,” you are “Holy Mary.”

Today I'd just like for us to look at that, concerning who a righteous person really is.

Righteousness Is a Gift

"Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."

Romans 5:1 (NKJV)

Right Standing With God

A righteous person is one who has right standing with God, made righteous by faith in what Jesus did on the cross. Being righteous has nothing to do with what you are doing. It is what God has done, and the fact that I have trusted and believed on God. So righteousness is a gift.

Part 2

Portraits of the Righteous

Noah, Joseph, Abraham, and Rahab

Noah: A Righteous Man Hears God

"By faith Noah, being divinely warned of things not yet seen, moved with godly fear, prepared an ark for the saving of his household... and became heir of the righteousness which is according to faith."

Hebrews 11:7 (NKJV)

God looked at a violent, wicked world and chose a just man who found grace in His eyes. You must be in right standing to hear the voice of God. Rain from the sky covering the whole earth had never happened before, yet for almost 120 years Noah and his family felled trees, built the boat, and preached righteousness while the city called him a crazy man.

Joseph: Someone God Could Trust

"Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not wanting to make her a public example, was minded to put her away secretly."

Matthew 1:19 (NKJV)

Why Pick a Carpenter?

If I were looking for somebody to protect my child against violent people, I wouldn't pick Joseph. But this was someone God could trust, who could hear and could obey. That's all God needs from someone who is righteous.

Abraham Believed God

"'Look now toward heaven, and count the stars if you are able to number them.' And He said to him, 'So shall your descendants be.' And he believed in the LORD, and He accounted it to him for righteousness."

Genesis 15:5-6 (NKJV)

Abraham had been waiting to give God a piece of his mind: “You took me from Ur. You said You'd bless me and make me a nation, seeing that I go childless? My servant inherits everything. You have not given me anything.” Righteous indignation. God answered with the stars and the sand, and Abraham believed. That belief was counted as righteousness.

Righteousness Is Not a Costume

Abraham didn't have to change his behavior first. No earring or no earring, head tie or no head tie, shoes off, white gown, stockings and gloves, a pious hat. Those things only make you look holy. The righteous are marked by something else:

They hear God

Right standing opens the ear to His voice

They trust God

“This is what God has said, and I believe it,” despite what everyone else says

They do things God's way

Bent on doing the will of God

Rahab in the Household of Faith

"By faith the harlot Rahab did not perish with those who did not believe, when she had received the spies with peace."

Hebrews 11:31 (NKJV)

I Was Wowed by It

God Himself could have at least covered the thing and said “the beautiful woman.” No: “the harlot,” because that's what she was when the spies came. She made up her mind, “I will hide you,” and God saw her as righteous. The Bible puts her in the same category as Abraham, Noah, and David.

Two Kinds of Righteousness

"But now the righteousness of God apart from the law is revealed... even the righteousness of God, through faith in Jesus Christ, to all and on all who believe... being justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus."

Romans 3:21-22, 24 (NKJV)

By the law

Trying to be right with God by what I do. No one can attain it. That is the “none righteous” of Romans 3:10.

Of God, by faith

Apart from the law, witnessed by the law and the prophets, to all who believe. He who knew no sin became sin so we could become the righteousness of God.

Can the Camera Come Closer?

I Have the Righteousness of God

“I don't really have any kind of righteousness of my own. I have the righteousness of God. I can't add to it. I can't subtract from it. It's a gift given to me.”

2 Corinthians 5:21

Part 3

Leave the Righteous Alone

A word to the wicked man who lies in wait

Like a Snake, Lying in Wait

The Lord revealed in prayer, through a true prophetic word, that there are people amongst us who do not want our good. You are here, but you are passing, murmuring, complaining. That's wickedness. That's almost like witchcraft.

They lie in wait, just waiting for Harvesters and Harvest to make a mistake: “I told you. I told you.” Like a snake, you come and lay there waiting to pounce. That is the wicked man of Proverbs 24:15.

The Word to the Wicked

Leave the righteous man alone. Leave Bishop Johnson alone. Keep your kongosa to yourself. Leave that man's wife alone. Leave that woman's husband alone. Leave us alone.

Part 4

The Righteous Get Back Up

Seven times down, seven times up

Your Mistake Will Not Identify You

"For a righteous man may fall seven times and rise again."

Proverbs 24:16 (NKJV)

The righteous man may fall, falter, make mistakes, even fail because of what the wicked are doing. You got married and the marriage did not work. You started a business and it did not go the way you wanted. Do not let your mistake identify you.

What identifies the righteous man is that he gets back up. He rises again. Just like Jesus: Jesus did not stay in the grave. Jesus got back up.

It's Getting-Back-Up Time

"For it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure."

Philippians 2:13 (NKJV)

While I was studying, a song came into my head: Don't be discouraged. You did it once and it did not work out. Do not stay down. Do not stop. For those who quit, whatever you stopped doing, it's time to get back up for your God. Your God is on your side.

Don't give up on God, for He won't give up on you. He put that desire and that vision inside of you. You don't want to fail, but give yourself an opportunity. You've got seven times.

If a Child Can Get Back Up

I watched all of my kids grow. The first time a child stands up, nobody is afraid; everybody is excited. Then the first step, everybody clapping. Then the child falls. You don't scold, you hug the child and pick the child up, because the child has reached the place where he or she wants to walk.

My daughter Rahma in her walker paid taxes to the floor. When she felt herself going, she straightened her leg and down she went. But when she got down there, she got back up. They cry, but they get back up. If a child can get back up, it's time for you to get back up.

You Give Too Much Power to One Person

Some have gotten discouraged in giving, in investing in Beyond. Whatever discouraged you, that is not what identifies you. No one should be that big. God wants to do something through you, and you let a puny human being stop you? God brought you to His church, and you let a puny human being take you out? No.

You can't keep a good man down. You are a get-back-upper. That's Paul's spirit: “I have fought the good fight.” You fight, you get back up. That's resurrection power working inside of you. You are not somebody who takes one knock and stays on the ground.

2 Timothy 4:7

Part 5

Enough Is Enough

The wicked falls by calamity, and the righteous arise

The Wicked Falls by Calamity

The righteous man

God on his side. God working in him, through him, and for him. Because of that, he can get up seven times.

The wicked man

Has said there is no God. Depends on his own strength, his own might, his own logic. He has no chance like the righteous: when he falls, he falls.

A Warning in the Name of Jesus

That spirit may be here, in God's house. Leave us alone. You have harassed us enough. We did not bother you, but we came this morning to say enough is enough.

At War With the Amalekite Spirit

"Because the LORD has sworn: the LORD will have war with Amalek from generation to generation."

Exodus 17:16 (NKJV)

The Amalekites fought dirty. They went to the back, where the older people and the young people were, and attacked from that angle. God rose up angry and said He would be at war with that spirit forever. Today I open my mouth against that spirit operating here: children having accidents, men and women in their prime gone to the netherworld.

Stand Up and Say It

Our finances

Leave our finances alone, in the name of Jesus.

Our health

Leave our health alone. We're not burying anybody here.

Our men and women

Leave our men and our women alone. No more prime people taken.

Our children

No more young person having accidents. It stops here. It stops now.

Enough is enough. We cancel it at every level. We command you to stop your works, in Jesus' name.

Let It Be Cut Here at Harvest

"With long life I will satisfy him, and show him My salvation."

Psalm 91:16 (NKJV)

The Forty-Day Fast

In Liberia, people were just passing in the church, sympathy from one family to the next. Pastor Arna Taylor arose and said, “We can't just sit here and let the enemy have his way with God's church.” The church called a forty-day fast, we prayed, and that thing was just cut. Let it be cut here. With long life the Lord will satisfy us.

God Doesn't Give You Boots for Nothing

"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age."

Ephesians 6:12 (NKJV)

They think that because of the word “righteous” you are pious and can't fight back. But we wrestle. That's a fight. The enemy brought his fight to your door, so you can't stay at home when the church is praying. I know you pray at home, but there is a corporate prayer. The righteous need to arise, come together, and put it down.

Draw a line: “Cross this line and I dare you.” You can say that because you have a Big Brother on this side. God is on your side. He is in you, and He wants to work through you.

High Five Somebody

You Are the Righteous

Don't let one little thing that you did, one small failure, stop you from pressing forward in the things of God. Get up. Get back up, because that's what we do. The Chief Shepherd got back up, and He is in you.

Get up and pray. Get up and fast. Get up and read the Word. Get up and return to church. He said, with all your shortcomings, you are the righteous.

Grace and Mercy

No Condemnation

"There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus."

Romans 8:1 (NKJV)

You may have thrown in the towel concerning a particular thing, and you heard the invitation to get back up. That drug problem keeps pulling you back; continue pressing in. You are welcome to identify as a righteous person. Just trust God. He is able to do exceeding abundantly above all you can ask, think, or imagine (Ephesians 3:20). You've had the vision for so long. Say what God has said, and God will bring it to pass.

Prayer Points

  • Father, thank You that I have been justified by faith and have peace with You; I receive righteousness as Your gift, not my achievement (Romans 5:1; Romans 3:24)
  • Deliver me from trying to earn right standing by rules and appearances; let me rest in the righteousness of God that is through faith in Jesus Christ (Romans 3:21-22; 2 Corinthians 5:21)
  • Like Noah and Joseph, make me someone You can trust to hear Your voice and obey, even when what You say has never happened before (Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 1:19)
  • Like Abraham, when the promise is delayed, let me bring my honest questions to You and still believe what You have said (Genesis 15:5-6)
  • Thank You that You counted Rahab among the household of faith; no past disqualifies the one who trusts You (Hebrews 11:31)

Prayer Points

  • I refuse to let my failure identify me; where I have fallen in marriage, business, giving, or faith, I get back up in the name of Jesus (Proverbs 24:16)
  • Lord, You are working in me both to will and to do; rekindle the desire and the vision You placed inside me, and let no puny human being stop it (Philippians 2:13)
  • We command every wicked spirit lying in wait against Harvest, against Bishop Johnson, and against our marriages to leave the righteous alone (Proverbs 24:15)
  • Enough is enough: leave our finances, our health, our men, our women, and our children alone; we break the Amalekite spirit and declare long life (Exodus 17:16; Psalm 91:16)
  • Raise up the righteous to arise, come together in corporate prayer, and put on the whole armor, because we wrestle against principalities and powers (Ephesians 6:12)
Declaration

We Are the Righteous

I am the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. I did not earn it and I cannot lose it by a costume; it is a gift I have received by faith. I hear God, I trust God, and I do things God's way. My mistakes do not identify me. I may fall seven times, but I rise again, because the Chief Shepherd who got back up lives in me. Wicked man, leave me alone. Leave my family, my finances, my health, and my church alone. Enough is enough. I will not give up on God, because He will not give up on me. I am a get-back-upper. I am the righteous. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Study Notes

Sermon Summary

The Righteous: Don't Play Around Them! - Pastor Zangai Peabody

Key points, principles, and takeaways

Overview

The Setting - With Bishop Johnson away in Australia for his birthday, Pastor Zangai Peabody preaches on a group of people the world does not understand: the righteous. The anchor text is Proverbs 24:15-16, read in the NKJV, the Amplified, and the NIV, and the opening word is aimed straight at any wicked person present: leave the righteous man alone. Don't plunder his resting place. Let the sleeping lion lie.

Who the Righteous Are - The world quotes Romans 3:10, “there is none righteous,” to call believers liars, bigots, and “holier than thou.” But that verse describes righteousness by the law, which no one attains. Romans 5:1 and Romans 3:21-25 reveal another righteousness, of God, apart from the law, given freely by grace to all who believe. Noah, Joseph, Abraham, and Rahab show that God has always counted ordinary, flawed people righteous because they heard Him, trusted Him, and did things His way. Righteousness is a gift: “I can't add to it, I can't subtract from it.”

What Marks Them - The righteous fall, but they get back up; the wicked fall by calamity and stay down. A failed marriage, a failed business, or discouragement in giving does not identify you. Like a child learning to walk, like Jesus who did not stay in the grave, the righteous rise again. It's getting-back-up time. The message ends in corporate warfare: the wicked who lie in wait, murmur, and gossip are warned, the Amalekite spirit behind accidents and premature death is confronted, and the church stands to declare “enough is enough” and long life (Psalm 91:16).

Key Scriptures

Proverbs 24:15-16 - “Do not lie in wait, O wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous; do not plunder his resting place; for a righteous man may fall seven times and rise again, but the wicked shall fall by calamity.” The anchor text, read in three versions. The Amplified's “uncompromisingly righteous, the one who is upright in right standing with God” supplies the definition; the NIV's “lurk like a thief” describes the wicked.

Romans 3:10 - “There is none righteous, no, not one.” The verse the world uses against believers. It is true of righteousness according to the law.

Romans 5:1 - “Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.” A righteous person is one who has right standing and peace with God through faith in the cross.

Genesis 6:8-9; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5 - Noah, a just man who found grace in God's eyes, built an ark for a flood that had never happened, and preached righteousness for almost 120 years while the city called him crazy.

Romans 3:21-25 - “But now the righteousness of God apart from the law is revealed... through faith in Jesus Christ, to all and on all who believe... being justified freely by His grace.” The resolution to Romans 3:10: two kinds of righteousness, and the second is a gift.

2 Corinthians 5:21 - He who knew no sin became sin, that we might become the righteousness of God in Him. “I don't have any kind of righteousness of my own. I have the righteousness of God.”

Romans 8:1; Ephesians 3:20 - No condemnation for those in Christ, and a God able to do exceeding abundantly above all we ask or think. The closing invitation to those who threw in the towel.

Summary 2 of 6

Main Teachings

The Misunderstanding - Growing up, every young believer hears Romans 3:10 quoted back at them: your own Bible says no one is righteous, so church people are liars and bigots, and holy women are “Holy Mary.” The sermon exists to correct that reading.

Righteousness Is Right Standing, and It Is a Gift - A righteous person has peace with God, justified by faith in what Jesus did on the cross. Being righteous has nothing to do with what you are doing; it is what God has done, plus the fact that you trusted and believed Him.

Three Marks of the Righteous - The righteous hear God (you must be in right standing to hear His voice), trust God (“this is what God has said, and I believe it,” despite what everyone else says), and do things God's way (bent on doing His will). Noah, Joseph, and Abraham each display this.

Not a Costume - Abraham did not have to change his behavior first. No white gown, no removing earrings, no head tie, no shoes off, no stockings and gloves, no pious hat. Those things only make people look holy. Rahab, a harlot when the spies arrived, was placed among the heroes of faith because she believed and acted.

Two Kinds of Righteousness - Righteousness by the law, which no one can attain, and the righteousness of God apart from the law, revealed through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, freely by His grace. The first explains Romans 3:10; the second is ours. “I can't add to it, I can't subtract from it. It's a gift given to me.”

The Wicked Lie in Wait - A prophetic word revealed people in the midst who do not want the church's good: present, but passing, murmuring, complaining, waiting for Harvesters and Harvest to make a mistake so they can say “I told you.” That is wickedness, almost like witchcraft. Like a snake, the wicked man lies there waiting to pounce. Leave Bishop Johnson alone, keep your kongosa to yourself, leave that man's wife and that woman's husband alone.

Your Mistake Will Not Identify You - The righteous man may fall, falter, and fail, sometimes because of what the wicked are doing. A marriage that did not work or a business that did not go as planned does not define you. The characteristic that identifies the righteous is that he gets back up, just as Jesus did not stay in the grave.

It's Getting-Back-Up Time - For those who quit and those who stopped: your God is on your side, working in you to will and to do. He put the desire and the vision inside you. Give yourself an opportunity; you have seven times. A child who falls while learning to walk is hugged, lifted, and gets back up without fear, and if a child can get back up, so can you.

You Give Too Much Power to One Person - Some are discouraged in giving and in investing in Beyond, or were pushed out of church by one person. No human being should be that big. God wants to do something through you, and you let a puny human being stop you? You can't keep a good man down. You are a get-back-upper, with resurrection power inside you.

The Wicked Falls by Calamity - The righteous man has God working in him, through him, and for him, so he can rise seven times. The wicked man has said there is no God and depends on his own strength, might, and logic. When he falls, he falls. The warning: leave us alone; you have harassed us enough.

Enough Is Enough - God swore perpetual war with Amalek, who attacked the old and young from behind. That spirit, operating in children's accidents and prime men and women gone to the netherworld, is commanded to stop. In Liberia, when people kept dying in the church, Pastor Arna Taylor called a forty-day fast and the thing was cut. “Let it be cut here at Harvest. We're not burying anybody here.”

The Righteous Fight - “Righteous” does not mean pious and passive. We wrestle, and wrestling is a fight. The enemy brought his fight to your door, so you cannot stay home when the church is praying; corporate prayer is where the righteous arise together and put it down. Draw the line, because you have a Big Brother on this side.

Summary 3 of 6

Principles

Righteousness is received, not achieved - It is a gift of right standing with God through faith in Christ, never a reward for behavior, dress, or religious appearance.

God counts trust and obedience as righteousness - Noah, Joseph, Abraham, and Rahab were ordinary and flawed; God called them righteous because they heard, believed, and acted on His word.

Right standing opens the ear - You must be in right standing with God to hear His voice, and hearing and obeying is all God needs from a righteous person.

Falling is not the mark of the wicked; staying down is - Both fall. The righteous rise again because God is working in them; the wicked fall by calamity because they rely on themselves.

Identity comes from what God has done, not from your worst day - Your mistake does not identify you. The ability to get back up does.

Honest questions do not disqualify faith - Abraham's righteous indignation ended in belief, and God counted the belief.

Opposition confirms the righteous - The wicked lie in wait precisely because the righteous have God on their side.

No person should outweigh God's call - Giving one human being the power to stop what God is doing through you gives them power they were never meant to have.

Corporate prayer carries authority - Private devotion matters, but a season of attack calls for the righteous to arise together, as the forty-day fast in Liberia showed.

The righteous are armed, not passive - God does not tell you to put on your boots for nothing. We wrestle against principalities and powers.

Summary 4 of 6

Warnings and Declarations

Warning: do not lie in wait - Murmuring, complaining, gossip (kongosa), and waiting for Harvest or its leaders to fail is wickedness, almost like witchcraft. The wicked man is told plainly: leave the righteous man alone.

Warning: do not let a failure define you - A failed marriage, a failed business, discouragement in giving, or a relapse is not your identity.

Warning: do not give one person too much power - No puny human being should be able to stop what God wants to do through you, or take you out of God's church.

Warning: do not mistake righteousness for passivity - They assume the righteous are too pious to fight back. We wrestle.

Warning: do not stay home when the church prays - The enemy brought the fight to your door; corporate prayer is the answer.

Warning to the wicked - You have said there is no God, so you depend on your own strength, might, and logic. You have no chance like the righteous man. When you fall, you fall by calamity.

Prophetic word - The Lord revealed in prayer that there are people in our midst who do not want our good. They are warned, in the name of Jesus, to leave Bishop Johnson, the marriages, and the church alone.

Declarations - “Leave our finances alone, in the name of Jesus.” “Leave our health, our men and our women alone.” “I open my mouth against the Amalekite spirit.” “Enough is enough. It stops here. It stops now. We cancel it at every level. We command you to stop your works in Jesus' name.” “Let it be cut here at Harvest. We're not burying anybody here. With long life the Lord will satisfy us and show us His salvation.” “No more young person having accidents.” “You are the righteous.” “Say what God has said; God will bring it to pass.”

Summary 5 of 6

Takeaways and Confessions

Receive the gift - Stop trying to add to or subtract from the righteousness of God. Identify as righteous because of what Christ did, not because of what you wear or do.

Hear, trust, obey - Be someone God can trust with His word, like Noah with the ark and Joseph with the child, even when what He says has never happened before.

Get back up - Whatever you quit or stopped doing, treat today as getting-back-up time. You have seven times.

Get up and pray, fast, read the Word, and return to church - The four practical commands from the platform.

Keep giving and investing in Beyond - Discouragement in giving is not what identifies you.

Take back the power you gave one person - Don't let a single human being stop God's work through you or push you out of His church.

Keep your kongosa to yourself - Refuse to murmur, complain, or lie in wait for another believer's fall.

Join the corporate prayer - Don't stay home when the church is praying. Arise with the righteous and put it down.

Press in without condemnation - If a drug problem or any habit keeps pulling you back, keep pressing. There is no condemnation in Christ, and you are welcome to identify as righteous.

Confessions - “I have the righteousness of God. I can't add to it, I can't subtract from it.” “My mistake will not identify me.” “I am a get-back-upper.” “Enough is enough.” “Don't give up on God, for He won't give up on you.” “You are the righteous.”

Closing prayer - “Father, we thank You for Your Word. We thank You. We are the righteous, in Jesus' mighty name.”

Dr. Nicku Mordi

Psalm 8 • Luke 2:52

A Donkey for Jesus

Why We Are Here

"Really I like to be a donkey for Jesus. Anywhere Jesus wants to go, I'm there to carry Him."

"Our purpose of being here is to win the lost at all cost."

Jesus Is Our Example

"And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men."

Luke 2:52 (NKJV)

One Verse

"It is a holistic blueprint for personal growth. It is our model for maturity. It balances intellectual, physical, spiritual, and social development."

Four Ways Jesus Grew

In wisdom

Mentally and intellectually.

In stature

Physically. "He didn't continue to wear baby shoes."

In favor with God

Spiritually. "He depended on God in everything He did."

In favor with men

Socially. Love, kindness, and respect to people wherever He was.

Why Should You Mature?

"Ask me why. I'm so glad you asked."

"Even God Himself desires for you, for me, to mature. That's why Jesus had to increase."

Out of the Mouth of Babes

"O Lord, our Lord, how excellent is Your name in all the earth, who have set Your glory above the heavens! Out of the mouth of babes and nursing infants You have ordained strength."

Psalm 8:1-2 (NKJV)

"He has ordained strength. Not weakness. They look so weak, but God's ways are not our ways."

Already Crowned

"What is man that You are mindful of him, and the son of man that You visit him? For You have made him a little lower than the angels, and You have crowned him with glory and honor."

Psalm 8:4-5 (NKJV)

The Hebrew reads "a little lower than Elohim," like God Himself. We are created in His image.

"Don't wait for other people to crown you. God has already crowned you with glory and with honor."

Created to Have Dominion

"You have made him to have dominion over the works of Your hands; You have put all things under his feet."

Psalm 8:6 (NKJV)

This is why we must mature: created to rule, to be an overcomer, to be His ambassador (Genesis 1:26).

"When God spoke to that clay, He said, take dominion. He didn't say you are cursed."

Four Things to Change

1. How we think and how we talk
2. Our appetite, our diet
3. Our wardrobe
4. Our focus: the blessings of God
Change 1

How You Think and Talk

"The putting away is your responsibility"

You Do the Putting Away

"When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I understood as a child, I thought as a child; but when I became a man, I put away childish things."

1 Corinthians 13:11 (NKJV)

"As a parent, you are responsible to take away your childish temper, put away your suspicions about other people."

The Blessed Twos

Speak Strength Over Your Children

"They were saying the terrible twos. I said no, because I've grown in my thinking. They are not the terrible twos, they are the blessed twos."

"When you keep punishing them, when you keep insulting them, they really don't grow. Instead they rebel."

"God, who created that child, has crowned him, her, with glory, with honor, with possibilities."

Think on These Things

"Whatever things are lovely, whatever things are of good report, if there is any virtue and if there is anything praiseworthy, meditate on these things."

Philippians 4:8 (NKJV)

"Say the things that are not as though they are" (Romans 4:17). "But you wouldn't be able to fulfill them if you don't know the word of God."

Change 2

Your Diet

"When you change your diet, you leave the junk"

Milk or Solid Food

"For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to teach you again the first principles of the oracles of God; and you have come to need milk and not solid food."

Hebrews 5:12 (NKJV)

"In this church, where we have teachers upon teachers, you continue to want milk?"

"Say, we are growing up."

Leave the Junk

  • "You spend your time watching all this in the media because it is fun. You are entertaining your mind."
  • "You are wasting time laughing at things that don't matter in the kingdom," and even forwarding them to other people.
  • "You are wasting time instead of eating meat, and eating solid food."

The Word of God Is Sweet

"Your words were found, and I ate them, and Your word was to me the joy and rejoicing of my heart."

Jeremiah 15:16 (NKJV)

"'Oh, the Bible is boring.' Who told you? Because you don't read, or you just read here and there, you think it is boring."

"How sweet are Your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth!" (Psalm 119:103)

Not by Bread Alone

"Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God."

Matthew 4:4 (NKJV)

"Not by your title. Not by your position. Not by what you have acquired. Not by the riches of this world."

"Human life is complete, enjoyable, fulfilling, if it aligns with the word of God."

Use the Little You Know

  • The Samaritan woman met Jesus once. One truth "transformed how she thought, transformed how she spoke," and she won her whole city (John 4).
  • "Don't say, I haven't gone to school. Don't say, I don't know anything. You already know too much."
  • At a party the night before: "Tomorrow, come to my church and dance." And the friend came.

Focus on the Blessings

  • Hunger for the Word. Pray to leave the junk and find the Word sweeter than honey. (Jeremiah 15:16, Psalm 119:103)
  • The right wardrobe. Put on Christ daily: tender mercies, kindness, humility, forgiveness, and love above all. (Romans 13:14, Colossians 3:12-14)
  • Peace in the storm. Let the peace of God rule your heart whatever comes. (Colossians 3:15)

Study Notes

Sermon Summary

We Purpose To Grow - Dr. Nicku Mordi

Key points, principles, and takeaways

Overview

Dr. Nicku Mordi presents Luke 2:52 as a one-verse blueprint for maturity: Jesus grew intellectually, physically, spiritually, and socially. From Psalm 8 she answers why believers must mature: God has already crowned us with glory and honor and created us to have dominion. To walk in that, four things must change: how we think and talk, our diet, our wardrobe, and our focus.

Context - Preached at Harvest Intercontinental Church, Olney, on Sunday, November 8, 2026, building on Bishop Johnson's "Grow Up" message (1 Corinthians 13:11, Hebrews 5:12) and its baby shoes. She ran out of time before fully developing the fourth point and closed by calling the pastors forward to pray for people.

Central claim - "Already, when you were born, God crowned you with glory and honor. Don't wait for other people to crown you."

Summary 1 of 9

Key Scriptures (1)

Luke 2:52 - "And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men." The introductory text and the model for balanced growth.

John 5:19 - "The Son can do nothing of Himself, but what He sees the Father do." Favor with God means depending on God in everything.

Psalm 8:1-6 - "Out of the mouth of babes and nursing infants You have ordained strength... You have crowned him with glory and honor. You have made him to have dominion." The main text: why we must mature.

Genesis 1:26 - "Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness; let them have dominion." Psalm 8 repeats what God said at creation.

1 Corinthians 13:11 - "When I became a man, I put away childish things." The putting away is your responsibility.

Romans 4:17 - God "calls those things which do not exist as though they did." How to speak over children.

Proverbs 22:6 - "Train up a child in the way he should go." You train by talking, not by insulting.

Philippians 4:8 - "Whatever things are lovely, whatever things are of good report... meditate on these things."

Summary 2 of 9

Key Scriptures (2)

Romans 13:12-14 - "Cast off the works of darkness... put on the Lord Jesus Christ." The wardrobe change, done daily at the mirror.

Hebrews 5:12-14 - "By this time you ought to be teachers... you have come to need milk and not solid food." The diet change.

Jeremiah 15:16 - "Your words were found, and I ate them." The Word is food.

Psalm 119:103 - "How sweet are Your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth!" The Word is not boring.

Matthew 4:4 and Luke 4:4 - "Man shall not live by bread alone." Not by title, position, possessions, or riches.

John 4:1-42 - The Samaritan woman. One truth from Jesus and she won her city.

Colossians 3:12-17 - "Put on tender mercies, kindness, humility, meekness, longsuffering... above all these things put on love... let the peace of God rule in your hearts."

Mark 4:35-41 - Jesus at peace in the storm while everyone was yelling.

Acts 12:1-10 - Peter asleep between soldiers the night before his execution. The angel removes the chains but tells Peter to put on his own sandals and garment.

Summary 3 of 9

Main Teachings (1)

A blueprint in one verse - Jesus grew in wisdom (mentally and intellectually), in stature (physically, "He didn't continue to wear baby shoes"), in favor with God (spiritually, depending on the Father) and in favor with men (socially, with healthy relationships). God desires the same balanced maturity for His people.

Why mature: you are crowned - Psalm 8 says God ordained strength out of the mouth of babes, made man a little lower than Elohim, crowned him with glory and honor, and gave him dominion. Maturity is growing into what God already placed on you: to rule, to overcome, to be His ambassador. Over the clay He said "take dominion," not "you are cursed."

Change 1: how you think and talk - Putting away childish things is your job. Parents especially must change their words: not "you foolish, you stupid," but calling things that are not as though they are. Her own children were "the blessed twos," not the terrible twos. Punishing and insulting produce rebellion, not growth. That troublesome child may be a president or an engineer.

Summary 4 of 9

Main Teachings (2)

Change 2: your diet - A church with "teachers upon teachers" should not still want milk. Spiritual junk is time spent on media that entertains the mind but does not matter in the kingdom. Solid food is the Word, which is sweet, full of everything the world borrows from it, and the only thing a human life can live by.

Change 3: your wardrobe - Baby shoes off; put on Christ and do not take Him off. Use the mirror each morning to say what God says about you. The elect wear tender mercies, kindness, humility, meekness, longsuffering, forgiveness, and above all love, and the peace of God then rules the heart, as it did for Jesus in the storm and Peter in prison.

God does His part, you do yours - The angel dropped Peter's chains, but Peter had to put on his own shoes. God will not do the witnessing for you.

Change 4: your focus - Focus on the blessings: you are alive, you are here, you are loved. Refuse to see yourself as ordinary, keep any pity party short, and fill your heart with thanks.

Grown people win the lost - A fed, changed believer sees everybody as family, starts simple conversations, and is led by the Spirit to the next person.

Summary 5 of 9

Principles

Maturity is holistic. Intellectual, physical, spiritual, and social, in balance.

God has already crowned you. Don't wait for other people to do it.

You were made for dominion. That is why you must grow up.

The putting away is your responsibility.

Words grow children or make them rebel. God ordains strength out of the mouth of babes.

Human life is complete, enjoyable, fulfilling, if it aligns with the word of God. Not because of marriage, a car, a title, or riches.

You don't have to know much. Use the simple things of God to win others.

When you put on Christ, you don't remove it. Choose it daily.

God will not do everything for you. Something is up to you to do.

You are an extension of God's love and peace. "It didn't come from me."

Summary 6 of 9

Warnings

Baby shoes, baby talk, baby wardrobe - Staying childish after years in a teaching church.

Insulting children - "You foolish. You stupid." Constant punishment and insult do not grow a child; they produce rebellion.

Spiritual junk food - Media that is fun but worthless in the kingdom, and time spent forwarding it to others.

Calling the Bible boring - It only seems so to those who do not read it, or read here and there.

Living on outside things - Title, position, acquisitions, and riches cannot make a life complete. "Some who are married are more lonely than the single."

Excuses - "I haven't gone to school. I don't know anything."

Waiting for God to do your part - The shoes do not go on by themselves.

Seeing yourself as ordinary - Depending on a culture that says so, and settling into self-pity.

Summary 7 of 9

Practical Takeaways

Use the mirror - Every morning, say what God says about you: "You are crowned with glory and honor."

Rename what you see - Blessed twos, not terrible twos. Speak possibility over your children and teach by talking.

Put away - Childish temper and suspicion of other people.

Cut the junk - Trade entertainment time for time eating the Word.

Fall in love with the Word - Read it properly, not here and there, until it is sweet.

Dress daily - Mercy, kindness, humility, forgiveness, love. Decide every day to walk in obedience.

Start a conversation - "Your tie is nice." Invite someone the way she invited her friend from the party.

Give thanks for little things - That you are alive, that you are here.

Come for prayer - "God, help me to change how I think, how I talk, what I spend time with."

Summary 8 of 9

Words Declared

Over the church and those online - "We are growing up. Even you on the line, wherever you are watching, you are growing up in the things of God."

Over every believer - "You are somebody. It doesn't matter what village you came from. He is your Father who loves you. He will do everything for you."

Over our children - "God, who created that child, has crowned him, her, with glory, with honor, with possibilities."

The closing prayer from Luke 2:52 - Grow in wisdom. In stature, past baby shoes, baby talk, and baby wardrobe. "We want you to know that you are favored by God."

Favor with men - "Know that everyone loves you. Even those that don't love you, don't worry. They haven't discovered it yet."

The hymn she read - "The Love of God," the first verse and refrain, in the church's year of unfailing love.

But you're doing it because you know God told you to do it, or because that's the need you see in the kingdom that needs to be done. And the results you're getting may not be very encouraging, because the response of the people is not what you hoped it would be. Hear me: you are not called to be responsible for *them*. You're responsible for *you*. You go ahead and keep being faithful. I'll keep preaching. You keep serving, church — wherever the need is, that which you have seen and identified in the kingdom that needs to be done, put your hand to the work. Remain faithful, whether or not those you are ministering to respond in any positive way, until God tells you to do something else. Keep doing what you are doing — in the name of Jesus. Hallelujah. Everybody say amen.

### Two Decisions

So I bring this to a conclusion, and there are two things I think we need to leave with — because I think this is where you need to make some decisions.

First of all: is there a King Uzziah in your life that is preventing you from seeing God, and from being able to say yes to God without having to negotiate? Let King Uzziah die. Surrender it. Release it, and let it go. God is better for you than King Uzziah. Let his throne be empty, and don't worry about it — because there's another throne, upon which sits the King of kings and the Lord of lords. There can only be one King in your life. There can be only one throne at the center of your life. Let all other thrones — everything else that is competing with God for preeminence, for influence, for the greatest influence on your decisions and on your behavior — anything that is competing with God and God's will for your life: let it go.

And here's the second thing: stop waiting for a specific assignment with your name on it in order to serve. There are needs in the kingdom. There are needs in the church. Find a need — a need that you know God has. Find a job that you know God wants done, that you can do — and do it. In Jesus' name. Amen.

### Closing Prayer

Father, I pray for Your people. I thank You that we're not hearers of the word only, but we're doers of Your word. I praise You that as we leave this place, we will do Your word by the grace that You give. We thank You that You have changed our "woe is me" to "here am I." Thank You. Like Isaiah, we pray that You give us a fuller revelation of Your holiness — that we might become humble before You, and consider serving You a privilege, and not a problem. To You be all the glory, in Jesus' name. Amen.
